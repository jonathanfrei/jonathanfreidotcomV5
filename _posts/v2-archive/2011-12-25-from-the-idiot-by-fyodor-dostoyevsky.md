We hurry and push and hustle, for the good of humanity! &lsquo;The world is becoming too noisy, too commercial!&rsquo; groans some solitary thinker. 'Undoubtedly it is, but the noise of waggons bearing bread to starving humanity is of more value than tranquillity of soul,&rsquo; replies another triumphantly, and passes on with an air of pride.

— from The Idiot by Fyodor Dostoyevsky
