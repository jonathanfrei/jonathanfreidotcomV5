[Kerry Skarbakka](http://www.guardian.co.uk/artanddesign/gallery/2013/mar/01/falling-man-kerry-skarbakka-pictures) makes the act of falling into a work of art.

> Arizona-based artist Kerry Skarbakka has received both awards and death threats for his controversial ‘falling man’ images.

This [set of images](http://www.guardian.co.uk/artanddesign/gallery/2013/mar/01/falling-man-kerry-skarbakka-pictures) gives me anxiety, but it captures a beautiful moment of panic. More of his work is [on his site](http://www.skarbakka.com/).
