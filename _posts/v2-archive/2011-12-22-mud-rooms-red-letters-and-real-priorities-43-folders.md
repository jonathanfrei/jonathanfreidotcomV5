A priority is observed, not manufactured or assigned. Otherwise, it&rsquo;s necessarily not a priority. Got that? You can&rsquo;t &ldquo;prioritize&rdquo; a list of 20 tasks any more than you can &ldquo;uniqueify&rdquo; 20 objects by &ldquo;uniqueness,&rdquo; or &ldquo;pregnantitze&rdquo; 20 women by &ldquo;pregnantness.&rdquo; Each of those words means something. An item is either unique or it is not. A woman is either pregnant or she is not. An item is either the priority or it is not. One-bit. Mutually exclusive. One ring to rule them all.
