With the recently announced [demise of Google Reader](http://googleblog.blogspot.com/2013/03/a-second-spring-of-cleaning.html) in Google’s Spring Cleaning post, it looks like Google is getting out of the blog reading business. Therefore, I’ve decided to transition away from [FeedBurner](http://feedburner.google.com), their blog feed publishing platform, as well. (_Goodbye RSS subscriber stats of questionable reliability_).

The new feed for this site has a link up in the header and [here](http://blog.jonathanfrei.com/rss). Please re-subscribe when you get the chance. If you use RSS, you’ll know what to do. (~~The old feed is still around, not deleted or broken, so it will still work as long as Google lets it.~~ **Update** : _Actually, I ended up having to delete the feed to get rid of the redirect loops_.)

If you regularly read RSS, you’re probably looking for a good [Google Reader alternative](http://lifehacker.com/5991272/most-popular-google-reader-alternative-feedly), but I (and many other sites) might add email subscriptions in the future. Email isn’t going anywhere. Let me know if you’d be into that.
