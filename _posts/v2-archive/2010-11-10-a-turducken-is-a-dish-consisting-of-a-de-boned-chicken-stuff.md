A turducken is a dish consisting of a de-boned chicken stuffed into a de-boned duck, which itself is stuffed into a de-boned turkey. The word turducken is a portmanteau of turkey, duck, and chicken or hen. (via [Turducken - Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turducken))
