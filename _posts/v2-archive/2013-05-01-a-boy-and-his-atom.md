# A Boy And His Atom

_The World’s Smallest Movie_

This short stop motion video, made by [IBM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oSCX78-8-q0&feature=player_embedded), captures individual atoms moving across the screen. That’s amazing to begin with, but what’s really incredible is that the researchers are moving and animating the individual atoms.
