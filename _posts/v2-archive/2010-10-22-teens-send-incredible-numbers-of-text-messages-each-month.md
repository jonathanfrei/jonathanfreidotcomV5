> New [data just released](http://blog.nielsen.com/nielsenwire/online_mobile/u-s-teen-mobile-report-calling-yesterday-texting-today-using-apps-tomorrow/) from Nielsen show the average teenager sends 3,339 text messages each and every month. More impressive, the average teenage female sends over 4,000 messages on a monthly basis. (via [Average Teenager Sends 3,339 SMS Messages Monthly, Average Teen Girl Sends 4,050 | Mobile Marketing Watch](http://www.mobilemarketingwatch.com/average-teenager-sends-3339-sms-messages-monthly-average-teen-girl-sends-4050-10318/?utm_source=feedburner&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=Feed%3A+MobileMarketingWatch+%28Mobile+Marketing+Watch%29))
