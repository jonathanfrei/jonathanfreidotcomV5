Like the coffee shop vibe but hate coffee? Need to focus, but it’s too quiet or the background noise is too distracting? [Coffitivity](http://www.coffitivity.com/) promises an ideal level of ambient noise to focus on work and get things done.

> Research shows it’s pretty hard to be creative in a quiet space. And a loud workplace can be frustrating and distracting. But, the mix of calm and commotion in an environment like a coffee house is proven to be just what you need to get those creative juices flowing.

[Rainy Mood](http://www.rainymood.com/) provides a similar calming background sound. And the benefits…they’re based on [_science_](http://www.jstor.org/discover/10.1086/665048?uid=3739936&uid=2&uid=4&uid=3739256&sid=21101759534431).
