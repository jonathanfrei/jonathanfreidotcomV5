Here’s a fun little poem that could easily be referring to the use of cell phones. Plenty of people feel this way if they misplace their phone…

**I Left My Head**

_BY LILIAN MOORE_

I left my head
somewhere
today.
Put it down for
just
a minute.
Under the
table?
On a chair?
Wish I were
able
to say
where.
Everything I need
is in it!
