The New Yorker has an interesting look at the [robotic side of Twitter](http://www.newyorker.com/online/blogs/elements/2013/11/the-rise-of-twitter-bots.html) and the challenges the service will face as it becomes a bigger business.

> As a service, Twitter’s greatest strength is that its users have total control over whom they follow and what they see. As a business, Twitter’s greatest opportunity is in violating that control with advertisements.
