The value of goals is not in the future they describe, but the change in perception of reality they foster. What we focus on changes what we notice. Our brain filters information, seeing one thing in a situation instead of something else, based on what we identify with, what we have our attention on, what we&rsquo;re looking for—more or less consciously.

— David Allen, [Productive Living](http://www.davidco.com/newsletters/archive/0212.html)
