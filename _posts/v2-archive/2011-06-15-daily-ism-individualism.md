This is the first post in a new series I’m starting on this blog. I call it the daily *ism. It’s going to be mostly information copied from Wikipedia, but each entry is going to feature another *ism.

Now *ism’s have a [variety of meanings](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/-ism), but at their root, they indicate a belief or principle. Now, I suppose there can be as many systems of though and belief as there are thinkers and believers. However, the credibility of some *ism’s are stronger than others, and unless you are a subscriber to [relativism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relativism) you will agree that some are more valid than others.

You wouldn’t believe how many *ism’s there are. With a quick Wikipedia Wildcard search, I came up with thousands of *ism’s to explore, which means I could be at this for a while.

This series isn’t meant to promote one *ism over another or to give a reflection of the *isms I espouse (many of them I reject entirely), but I will gravitate towards ones I find interesting. Each *ism will also include links to other *ism’s to explore.

So without further introduction, here’s the first *ism, individualism, an *ism which I happen to be quite proud to hold.

> **Individualism** is the [moral](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral "Moral") stance, [political philosophy](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_philosophy "Political philosophy"), ideology, or social outlook that stresses “the moral worth of the [individual](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individual "Individual")”.[[1]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-britannica.com-0) Individualists promote the exercise of one’s goals and desires and so [independence](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence "Independence") and [self-reliance](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-reliance "Self-reliance")[[2]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-thefreedictionary.com-1) while opposing most external interference upon one’s own interests, whether by [society](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society "Society"), [family](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family "Family") or any other group or [institution](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institution "Institution").[[2]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-thefreedictionary.com-1)
>
> Individualism makes the individual its focus[[1]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-britannica.com-0) and so it starts “with the fundamental premise that the human individual is of primary importance in the struggle for liberation.” [Classical liberalism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_liberalism "Classical liberalism") (including [libertarianism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libertarianism "Libertarianism")), [existentialism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existentialism "Existentialism") and [anarchism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anarchism "Anarchism") (especially [individualist anarchism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualist_anarchism "Individualist anarchism")) are examples of movements that take the human individual as a central unit of analysis.[[3]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-ReferenceA-2)
>
> It has also been used as a term denoting “The quality of being an individual; individuality”[[2]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-thefreedictionary.com-1) related to possessing “An individual characteristic; a [quirk](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idiosyncrasy "Idiosyncrasy").”[[2]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-thefreedictionary.com-1) Individualism is thus also associated with [artistic](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art "Art") and [bohemian](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemianism "Bohemianism") interests and lifestyles where there is a tendency towards self creation and experimentation as opposed to tradition or popular mass opinions and behaviors[[2]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-thefreedictionary.com-1)[[4]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-jstor.org-3) as so also with [humanist](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanism "Humanism") philosophical positions and ethics.[[5]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-4)[[6]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism#cite_note-5)
