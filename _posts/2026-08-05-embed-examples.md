## Notes

- The URL must sit alone on a line (blank lines above/below).
- Inline links in paragraphs are left alone.
- Disable for a page with front matter: `url_embeds: false`.
- Supported: YouTube, Vimeo, X/Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Spotify, CodePen.
