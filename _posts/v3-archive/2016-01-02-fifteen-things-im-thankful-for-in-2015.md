Last year I wrote a post of <a href="https://jonathanfrei.com/2015/01/fourteen-things-im-thankful-for-from-2014">Fourteen things I’m thankful for in 2014</a>. This has been another year filled with new challenges and joys, and I’m glad to be able to share another list of blessings. Here are some of the things I’m thankful for from 2015:

<ol>
    <li>For my wife who took good care of me all year, especially during the difficult times where she faithfully kept the ‘for worst’ part of our vows</li>
    <li>For a vacation with good friends to Cancun to celebrate friendship and an end of a hard summer</li>
    <li>For getting a <a href="http://www.gartner.com/events/">great new job</a> with wonderful coworkers at an amazing company just when I needed it most</li>
    <li>For taking my family to my <a href="https://facebook.com/levikircher/albums/10156490275085508/">sister Kelly’s wedding</a> and spending time with my parents, sister, and new brother-in-law</li>
    <li>For a new iPad (for reading comics) and new iPhones (for everything else)</li>
    <li>For how the <a href="http://imdb.com/title/tt2488496/">Star Wars movie</a> reminded me of the great things of my childhood and gave me the opportunity to share that joy with my kids</li>
    <li>How <a href="http://imdb.com/title/tt1392190/">Mad Max</a> reminded me to confront problems rather than drive yourself crazy trying to run from them</li>
    <li>For fun work trips to exotic (and not so exotic) locations — Stamford, London, Orlando, Dallas</li>
    <li>For heros like <a href="http://pre04.deviantart.net/0231/th/pre/f/2015/318/1/a/rey_and_bb8_imax__hi_res_textless_wallpaper__by_aracnify-d9gl4r1.png">Rey</a> and <a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/-lDHgHmEXKgI/VgoLFhwGA-I/AAAAAAAAWlo/bwLbeZiSXu8/s1600/supergirl-melissa-benoist-supergirl-tv-show.jpg">Kara</a> for my daughter to look up to in popular media.</li>
    <li>For my oldest son’s amazing progress in learning to read (he has a great <a href="https://facebook.com/gabriela.frei">teacher</a>)</li>
    <li>For my younger son’s unending sweetness</li>
    <li>For my baby girl becoming less of a baby and more of a <a href="https://jonathanfrei.com/2015/09/tbt-natalies-baptism">girl</a></li>
    <li>For finding a joy in <a href="https://appsto.re/us/1Zohx.i">running</a> and pushing my body to its limits</li>
    <li>For another year of <a href="https://jonathanfrei.com/2015/04/5-years-of-a-non-conformist-commute">avoiding a traditional commute</a> and biking to work</li>
    <li>For a ridiculously fun <a href="https://www.facebook.com/gabriela.frei/posts/876843283320">Christmas</a> and New Year’s Eve with the family</li>
</ol>

This really was an exceptional year, and I’m looking forward to the challenges and joys of 2016.
