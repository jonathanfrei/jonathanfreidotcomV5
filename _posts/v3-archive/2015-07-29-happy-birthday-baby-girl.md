My little girl turned two today. She has gotten <i>so</i> big <i>so</i> fast. It seems like she was just a baby—fitting snugly into the crook of my arm—but now she has grown into a beautiful little girl. She was the smallest of my three kids at birth but now has no trouble keeping up with her older brothers' shenanigans.

We were a family before she came to join us, but I can't imagine how we could be a family without her. She makes our family whole.
