You clicked ‘accept’ without reading the terms. It’s okay, nobody reads the terms. You’re in a hurry to use the app you just signed up for.

Most people accept the terms without having any idea about what they are agreeing to. [Terms of Service; Didn’t Read](https://tosdr.org/index.html) aims to fix that by assigning rating to popular websites and apps as well as labeling and explaining various clauses their TOS.

> We are a user rights [initiative](https://tosdr.org/about.html "Learn more about us") to [rate and label](https://tosdr.org/classification.html "What's our classification?") website terms & privacy policies, from very good Class A to very bad Class E.
>
> Terms of service are often **too long to read** , but it’s important to understand what’s in them. Your rights online depend on them. We hope that our ratings can help you get informed about your rights.
