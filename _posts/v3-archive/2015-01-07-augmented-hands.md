I do not want to try this <a href="http://flong.com/projects/augmented-hand-series/">machine that changes how you perceive your hands</a>, but seeing what is possible with augmented reality is amazing.

> The "Augmented Hand Series" (by <a href="http://flong.com/" target="_blank">Golan Levin</a>, <a href="http://csugrue.com/" target="_blank">Chris Sugrue</a>, and <a href="http://kylemcdonald.net/" target="_blank">Kyle McDonald</a>) is a real-time interactive software system that presents playful, dreamlike, and uncanny transformations of its visitors' hands. It consists of a box into which the visitor inserts their hand, and a screen which displays their 'reimagined' hand—for example, with an extra finger, or with fingers that move autonomously. Critically, the project's transformations operate within the logical space of the hand itself, which is to say: the artwork performs "hand-aware" visualizations that alter the deep structure of how the hand appears.
