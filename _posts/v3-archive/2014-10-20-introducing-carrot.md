Many landing pages for new tech products follow a very standard layout and structure. It's one that <a href="http://www.apple.com/iphone-6/">Apple popularized</a>, and that others have <a href="https://www.uber.com/">copied</a> <a href="http://www.fitbit.com/flex">repeatedly</a>.

<a href="http://www.introducingcarrot.com/">Introducing Carrot</a> pokes fun at the trope, showcasing the familiar vegetable in the popular product marketing format of the day. Here's the pitch:

<blockquote>Carrot is designed with you in mind. It's a seamless experience, meticulously crafted, from beginning to end. It's not just a vegetable, it's what a vegetable should be.</blockquote>

This is some clever work by <a href="https://twitter.com/lucciinthesky">Dan Angelucci</a>, who says it will "<a href="https://twitter.com/lucciinthesky/status/519476538455392256">massively disrupt the vegetable sector</a>." (HT <a href="https://adactio.com/links/7695">Adactio</a>)
