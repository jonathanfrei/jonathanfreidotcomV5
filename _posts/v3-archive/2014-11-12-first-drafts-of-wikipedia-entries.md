<a href="http://firstdraftsofhistory.tumblr.com">First Drafts of History</a> is a Tumblr that collects screen shots of the first versions of various Wikipedia entries.

Some of them are <a href="http://firstdraftsofhistory.tumblr.com/post/101377474472/article-nasa-revision-24-september-2001">humorously incomplete</a> and <a href="http://firstdraftsofhistory.tumblr.com/post/102407850177/article-buzzfeed-revision-11-august-2009">surprisingly accurate</a>.
