Like many others, I've been bingeing on the latest season of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_(U.S._TV_series)">House of Cards</a> over the past several days. This morning I woke up with an idea for a crossover between House of Cards and another show I've been guilty of bingeing on--<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24_(TV_series)">24</a>. The idea wasn't for a full series or episode--just for a single scene.

The scene opens at the top of the back stairwell in the White House with <a href="https://images1.fanpop.com/images/photos/1300000/Season-Three-Jack-Bauer-24-1341969-500-303.jpg">Jack Bauer</a> interrogating <a href="http://www.standard.co.uk/incoming/article9165676.ece/alternates/w620/33HOUSECARDSMAIN0303A.jpg">President Frank Underwood</a>.

"Who else knows about this," Bauer demands, referring to some dubious plot Underwood concocted.

"Just you," says Underwood, unfazed.

"Who else knows!" shouts Bauer, pushing Underwood against the railing.

Underwood does not look afraid, just annoyed. He <a href="http://pixel.nymag.com/imgs/daily/vulture/2015/gifs/frank-sideeye-011.w529.h352.gif">glances at the camera</a> for a moment--breaking the fourth wall.

Bauer, unlike every other character in the series, notices the glance and comes closer to the camera. He looks directly at the audience.

"You stood by and let this happen!? I will find you."

The screen goes black, and the episode ends.

And now I'm a guy that writes fan fiction.
