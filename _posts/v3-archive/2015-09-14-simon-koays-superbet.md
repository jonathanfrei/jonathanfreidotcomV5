My&nbsp;kids and I really enjoyed this <a href="http://www.simonkoay.com/#/superbet/">superhero alphabet</a> by illustrator <a href="http://www.simonkoay.com/about/">Simon Koay</a>. They were able to identify almost all the comic book characters, which is impressive for a six and three-year old.&nbsp;

<a href="http://www.simonkoay.com/#/superbet/"><img class="alignnone" src="http://static1.squarespace.com/static/534b8bcce4b022b8c6380139/534e8537e4b0757a37397f4b/555ffed7e4b05dcc48235240/1432354540601/?format=1000w" alt="Superbet "  /></a>

Here is Koay's description of the project:

<blockquote>'Superbet' is a typographic exploration of alphabet design by reimagining each letter of the alphabet as everyone's favourite superheroes and supervillains. Each letter has been created with inspiration from characters who share that same initial in their name.</blockquote>
