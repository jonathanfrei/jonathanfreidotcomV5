> Music can lead us beyond the threshold of mystery. Just as someone following a stream eventually reaches its source, the beauty of song can guide us towards God, satisfying the thirst for meaning and happiness that the world itself cannot quench.
>
> This was the image Pope Leo XIV offered in his reflection during the Canticle of Peace gathering, held this evening, 29 July, at Borgo Laudato si' in the Pontifical Gardens of Castel Gandolfo, beneath the shade of the ancient holm oak known as "Methuselah."
