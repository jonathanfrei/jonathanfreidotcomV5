This is the first post on the new version of the site.

Built with Jekyll, deployed by GitHub Actions, and served behind Cloudflare. The design is intentionally minimal so it can grow into custom article layouts, a portfolio, or a photo gallery later without fighting the foundation.

Drafts live in an obfuscated folder that is excluded from the public build, indexes, feeds, and robots.txt.
