Title: Getting Better vs. Getting Better at Getting Better
---

**Core question:** When does improvement become recursive?

**Thesis:** There is a meaningful difference between improving an output, improving the behavior that produces it, and improving the process used to discover and implement improvements.

**Key examples:** A programmer fixes a bug; a team improves its testing process; an individual changes how they practice; an organization changes how it evaluates processes.

**Important distinction:** Recursion is not simply repetition. The object of improvement must include some part of the mechanism that produces future improvement.

**Bridge:** Establish the conceptual vocabulary needed for the software, individual, organizational, and political cases that follow.
