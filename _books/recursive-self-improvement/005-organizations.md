---
Title: Organizations and Complex Adaptive Systems
---

**Core question:** Why is improving a company harder than improving software?

**Thesis:** Organizations contain many agents with different objectives, delayed feedback, emergent behavior, institutional memory, and incentives that can change in response to the improvement process itself.

**Key concepts:** Organizational learning, local versus global optimization, emergence, path dependence, institutional inertia, and feedback delays.

**Tension:** A process designed to improve the organization can itself become bureaucracy.

**Bridge:** Markets amplify the interaction between independent adaptive systems.
