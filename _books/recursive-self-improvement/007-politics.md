---
title: "Politics: A System That Can Change Its Own Rules"
---

**Core question:** Can a political system recursively modify its own decision mechanism?

**Thesis:** Democratic systems can change not only policies but the rules and institutions through which future policies are selected.

**Key examples:** Elections, electoral reform, legislation, constitutional amendments, institutional redesign, and changes to voting rules.

**Tension:** The system must use its existing rules to decide how to change those rules. The optimizer and the optimization mechanism are entangled.

**Bridge:** Evolution provides a radically different form of cumulative improvement without centralized intentional design.
