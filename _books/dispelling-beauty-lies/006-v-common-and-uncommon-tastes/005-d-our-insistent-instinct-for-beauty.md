Women sometimes trick themselves into believing their innate sense of beauty matches the fashionable one, while the so-called “genuine feminine beauty” presented in this article is just something men try to impose on them against their will. The romance covers above reveal that this self-analysis is backward.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/calvin-1.jpg)

_Not what women (or straight men) want._

The unattractive ideal of the fashion world is the one men have imposed on them. Gay men, that is. And generations of relentless marketing have pressured women to mistake this indoctrination for intuition.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/gaffney.jpg)

_It's time to let yourself dream again._

The fashion world has created a negative-sum game with arbitrary rules. They grant women status points for achieving a boyish look that's both taxing to maintain and unattractive to the majority of the population. But when free to dream their own private dreams, women would rather have a curvaceous, feminine figure that satisfies the common male taste. They want to be loved and lusted after, not just held up as trophies for public display.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/model2.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/09/runway2024c.jpg)

_These are real runway models that appeared in Vogue and Elle. Shoulder pads increase the shoulder-hip ratio, a primary component of masculine beauty, instead of improving the waist-hip and waist-breast ratios, primary components of feminine beauty. Is it any wonder fashion designers like them so much?_

You might assume that because men have an innate instinct for feminine beauty, they're completely unaffected by the flood of aesthetic propaganda from the media and beauty industry. But that's not quite how instinct works. Instinct only reveals itself in the course of experience. It asserts itself separately from and often later than the social learning that teaches young people what they're “supposed” to like.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/classr2.jpg)

_Don't trust everything you learn in school, kids._

Despite its vulgarity, the humorous post we'll look at next illustrates this well. Its author has been bombarded by media valorization of thinness for his entire life. He seems to be unaware that the near totality of art history portrayed thick women as the most beautiful.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/le-bain-turc-1862.jpg)

_Le Bain Turc_ , 1862.

So when instinct finally informs him in no uncertain terms that he prefers thick to thin, he feels bewildered by the conflict between what he was taught he should feel and what he actually feels.

_The level of propaganda in the media is so high that even men experience confusion. Eventually their own instincts punch them in the face. One reason surveys don't match revealed preferences is that many men automatically give the answers they were taught to without any introspection into their own feelings, choices, or behavior._

Some men fail to pass through the stage of personal growth that resolves this conflict. So they remain stuck with one foot in what the media taught them, and another in what their instincts tell them. They may even seek wives who send socially approved signals that turn them off sexually. Their claims and behaviors become contradictory in a way that must be as confusing for them as it is for the women who have to deal with them. This stunted development amounts to a form of the Madonna-whore complex, which I'll address in detail in a later chapter.
