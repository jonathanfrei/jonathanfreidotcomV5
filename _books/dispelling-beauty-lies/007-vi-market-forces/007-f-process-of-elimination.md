Before we continue, let's pause to summarize the main points of this section. Median male tastes aren't a perfect guide to attractiveness. Women with rare traits that satisfy minority tastes are valued even if they're unpopular. And often they're more popular than women who match median tastes exactly. Because people neglect to consider market forces, they mistakenly believe the most advantageous appearance falls closer to the average than it really does.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/10/redhead3.jpg)

_More popular than you think._

So market forces make beauty more complicated than it first seemed. And there's a further difficulty as well. It's possible we've been misled into overlooking some types of beauty that are meaningfully different from the common male taste, but almost as popular and desirable.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/mintgirl.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/aig-brown-thigh.jpg)

_Would they mislead you?_

I'll explain with a metaphor. Suppose ice cream is everyone's favorite dessert. That's well and good, but if we fixate _only_ on ice cream, we'll overlook other desserts that are also widely loved. For instance, most people enjoy chocolate cake even though it's quite different from ice cream. Perhaps women are like desserts, and come in many varieties that differ from the most desirable one, but are still tasty.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/flour-rear-crop-1.jpg)

_Another flavor._

How can we account for these other varieties of beauty? The answer is lurking in an unexpected place. Namely, harem animes.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/harem-235.jpg)

Harem animes have a big cast of characters specifically intended to satisfy men's love of variety. These extend well beyond the most popular taste, and comprise every type of feminine beauty that interests a reasonable fraction of men. To continue the dessert metaphor, they include not only ice cream, but also chocolate cake _and_ cinnamon buns _and_ twinkies, and pretty much everything else too.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/interspecies2-crop.jpg)

_The kitchen sink of romantic prospects. Don't overlook the fairy on the table._

With so many female characters, the important information isn't what harem animes include. It's what they leave out. Because they're trying to show as much variety as possible, they only leave out types that are distinctly _un_ attractive.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/Monmusu2.webp)

This gives us an opportunity to analyze feminine beauty in a broader way than we did earlier. Instead of pinpointing a single, narrow ideal, we can now demarcate the complete range of attractiveness by excluding the omissions, much like we can draw an outline of the continents by tracing the oceans.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/harem-63426.jpg)

_Draw the negative space._

There are four prominent omissions from harem animes. The first and most obvious is the apple figure (the reverse of the hourglass). This rotund figure has so little appeal that even when men are bored of every conventional type of beauty and looking for more variety, they don't find it interesting at all.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/haremanime2.jpg)

_What's missing from this picture?_

The second omission is flat women who look older than twenty. Flat women who look younger than twenty are normally included in harem animes. As we've already seen, this age effect is a consequence of the interplay between curves and neoteny.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/harem7.jpg)

_Very long hair is much more common in imaginary women than in reality. This supply-demand advantage makes it a valuable trait._

There are hundreds of harem animes, so occasional exceptions wouldn't be surprising. And the genre isn't a faultless guide to beauty. But the four omissions I've listed are both quite consistent, and confirmed by low frequency in other imaginary women. We can therefore be confident that short hair, rotundness, spindliness, and flatness in adults are unattractive to the large majority of men.

While market forces and the diversity of tastes do make feminine beauty more complicated, this analysis of unattractiveness restores clarity about fundamentals. Certainly there's more than one way to be beautiful, and many uncommon and rare tastes exist. Nevertheless, the large majority of men insist on some basic points.

> _Anecdotal Illusions_
>
> To understand how beauty is perceived and valued by men as a whole you need to think statistically _and_ consider market forces. Most people struggle to do one, let alone both. So it's tempting to form your views on the basis of anecdotes. For instance, “Some guy online said a celebrity was fat in an especially hurtful way.” Anecdotes like this stand out and feel psychologically powerful, but they're often misleading. One man's trash is another man's treasure. On top of that, trolls and influencers say things that aren't true just for views. _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ analyzes attractiveness statistically so you can see the real patterns behind it. Anecdotes aren't completely meaningless, but you need to recognize them for what they are and assign them low importance in comparison.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/bus-girl.jpg)

_Don't let the opinions of randos shape your world or ruin your day._
