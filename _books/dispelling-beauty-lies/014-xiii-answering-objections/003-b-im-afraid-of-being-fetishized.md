Another concern related to objectification is “fetishization.” Some women have a rare trait or exotic heritage that men find particularly attractive. This might feel a little strange, especially when the trait in question seems meaningless to you. However, _many_ aspects of sex appeal are arbitrary and meaningless if you consider them objectively. As long as your lover appreciates you as a person too, it's not a reason to worry.

Exoticism itself can be a source of interest and sex appeal. For example, men tend to find foreign accents intriguing. Furthermore, foreign traits may become more valuable than they were at home simply due to differences in supply and demand. This kind of sexual arbitrage is [free money](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/03/arbitrage2.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), and it's [very silly](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/03/silly-1.png?ref=jsanilac.com) not to take it.

In fact, having an unusual trait that some men particularly like is a big advantage in your favor, as I've [already explained](../007-vi-market-forces/001-vi-market-forces.md) many times. It gives you an edge over other women, and helps you catch a better mate than you could without it. It doesn't make sense to handicap yourself by throwing away that edge because some guys like it "too much."

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/09/demi-coconut-1.jpg)

_This "fetishization" worry is sometimes pushed by female competitors to keep women with standout traits from exploiting their advantages. "Avoid men who fetishize your standout traits" is advice in the same vein as "don't sell your beach house to people who like the beach."_

So instead of being ashamed of your rare trait or exotic background, I recommend you [lean into it](../007-vi-market-forces/006-e-arbitrage-and-standout-traits.md). Especially when you're alone with your lover. You'll have better results, higher self-esteem, and more fun too.

_Don't shoot yourself in the foot because your feet are "too cute." You catch the best mates by taking full advantage of your strengths, not by avoiding the men who appreciate them the most. It's almost certain that the best mate you can catch will be precisely one of these men. That's how matching works. Intentionally seeking out men who don't love your standout trait and avoiding men who do is just self-sabotage._
