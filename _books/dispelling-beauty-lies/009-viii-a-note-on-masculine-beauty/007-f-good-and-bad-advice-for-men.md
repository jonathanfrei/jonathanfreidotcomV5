Male readers are hoping I'll conclude this section with tricks that will help them to catch women. I'd very much like to. However, the facts compel me to advise fatalism.

Of the three main factors in male bodily attractiveness, only muscular strength is under your control, and even that to a limited degree. Fashions are bland, and makeup is out of the question. There are some effective options for facial cosmetic surgery that I haven't time to discuss here. But overall, men's ability to improve the appearance they inherited is considerably less than women's.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/aig-glare-crop.jpg)

_All the major dimensions of feminine beauty can be meaningfully improved with makeup, clothing choices, and cosmetic surgery, but this isn't true of masculine beauty._

> _Unequal Returns On Investment In Beauty_
>
> After they cover physical basics like a healthy bodyfat level and weightlifting, men get a bigger boost to their attractiveness by working to increase their reputation, charisma, and wealth than by obsessing about details of their appearance. Feminine beauty is much more malleable than masculine beauty, and it influences attractiveness more too. So women get a bigger boost by working harder to improve their appearance and perfect visual details. These different incentives will produce different outcomes even if other factors are equal.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/07/Alexander_Rothaug_-_The_Three_Fates-_circa_1910.jpg)

Men looking for a magic solution to their limited control over physical appearance sometimes turn to pick-up artist “game” (a collection of psychological tricks for seducing women). I won't claim that game is entirely false.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/vogueick.png)

_Some women will test you by playing little psychological games just to see how you react. It's important not to respond in a way that reveals weakness or submissiveness._

However, the effect size is limited, and those who peddle it are often guilty of overpromising. The next time you walk down a busy street, observe the couples. They have a similar level of purely physical attractiveness. If psychological tricks could really cancel out a poor appearance, this wouldn't be the case. The exceptions are so rare and noticeable that they prove the rule, and they typically involve fame or fortune.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/aig-gold.jpg)

> _Midas Touch_
>
> Wealth is indeed an attractive feature. However, unlike other attractive features, it comes with a “winner's curse.” Wealth causes your pool of potential mates to include a far higher fraction of women who care more about your money and the lifestyle you can provide than they care about you as a person. Dodging this issue by pretending to be poorer, or only dating wealthy women, would also cancel out the advantage. Because [wealthy men are magnets for mercenary women](https://www.jsanilac.com/billionaire/), they need to use great care when choosing long-term mates, and even then, the risk of getting it wrong will remain elevated. That's why wealthy men as a group tend to have more negative views on women than men who are equally attractive for other reasons. So money isn't quite as valuable in practice as it is on the paper of paperback romances.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/dressred2.jpg)

_Would you trust her?_

I'll leave men with a short list of advice.

> 1. Maintain a healthy weight. 2. Develop your upper body strength. 3. Approach many women and don't take rejection to heart, because it's impossible to know in advance who will particularly [like your face](../008-vii-the-most-important-secret-about-faces/001-vii-the-most-important-secret-about-faces.md). 4. It's good to love women, but loving a woman is trouble. Don't allow your emotions to fixate on one woman before she shows strong evidence of commitment and reliability. 5. Character does matter. Read romance novels to learn which traits women find attractive. These are not the same as the traits society praises as virtuous. 6. Once you've done the above, accept that the rest is up to fate. Believing you control things you don't is a recipe for unhappiness.

_If you approach them, five of these women will laugh at you and reject you cruelly in front of your friends. But one of them will marry you. There's no way to know which are which in advance. What will you do?_

The third point in the list deserves special emphasis, because today many otherwise decent young men neglect it and suffer the consequences. When you approach women in your league, rejections will be more common than matches, and there's no way to avoid this with premeditation and calculation. To optimize your outcome you must have the courage to repeatedly put your self-esteem at risk, and persist despite the inevitable demoralizing rejections. If you do not do this and instead aim to minimize rejections, you'll underperform your potential.

_More classic depictions of women's fantasies. The hero overcoming the heroine's initial resistance is a perennial feature of romance novels, and romantic comedies as well._

Rejection isn't always genuine, but sometimes a test to see if you'll insist. By insisting, you demonstrate that your interest isn't fickle, and also make her feel more desirable. Whether a rejection is definitive or leaves the door open to further courtship is something you'll need to decide with good judgment. It's also smart to know when to stop.
