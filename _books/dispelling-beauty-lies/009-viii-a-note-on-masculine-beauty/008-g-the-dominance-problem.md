As a side note, fantasies of dominance and violence are much [more](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/BivonaDissertation2008.png?ref=jsanilac.com) [common](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/bdsmstats.png?ref=jsanilac.com) among women than men. In fact, they don't even make the [top fifteen list](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/size/w1000/2023/09/Joyal-Cosette-Lapierre-2014-.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) of men's fantasies. Women aren't aware of this gap, and those who project a lust for sexual violence onto men in order to enhance their own jouissance don't really want to be aware. Some women may expect you to engage in a theater of dominance that doesn't come naturally, and even detracts from your enjoyment. Whether you choose to indulge them is up to you. In my article “[The Illusion of Dominance](https://www.jsanilac.com/dominance/)” I discuss this issue in detail, and also identify a fundamental error of “game” and “redpill” seduction techniques.

This romance author sold 60 million novels on themes such as being captured and ravished by Viking warlords, being disenfranchised by seven-foot tall aliens who look like Fabio, and being [enslaved and seduced in the exotic sands of the Orient](https://www.jsanilac.com/chapter-i-the-joy-of-slaving/). In her novels physical attraction is of primary importance, and love never precedes sex. While it's not clear she represents the majority taste, there's no denying that her work was popular with women.
