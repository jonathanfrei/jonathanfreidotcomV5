Nevertheless, to maintain a balanced mind romantic notions are best tempered by colder truths.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/demi-snow-2.webp)

I also asked men to choose their favorites from the set of facial composites below. These superimpose many faces to filter out details and blemishes and depict general types. To rank them I gave each man's first choice three points, his second choice two points, and his third choice one point. The totals are recorded above the faces. A second poll of the top three faces given on a separate occasion confirmed the same order of popularity.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/faces-scored-sand3.jpg)

_Men were instructed to examine the chart of facial composites starting from a random face and list their three favorites in order (n=286). A higher score indicates more men chose her as a favorite._

I've already said that surveys aren't trustworthy. And for reasons I'll explain in a moment, faces are particularly intractable. So I'm not presenting these scores to create a classification system or determine the “best” facial type. Instead they're valuable because they reveal a larger pattern in facial preferences.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/gene1.jpg)

In the first half of this section we observed that each man has a unique ideal he loves the most. But the lopsided scores in the chart show these preferences aren't evenly distributed. Some facial types attract many more suitors than others. And that remains the case after blemishes, disfigurements, and asymmetries are corrected.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/aig-snow-11.jpg)

So making small improvements to create the “best version” of a given face isn't enough to make that face popular. Its maximum popularity is largely predetermined by its basic architecture, which is something most women would be reluctant to change even if they could.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/hande12.jpg)

Why does popularity matter? The least popular face in the survey only had one suitor among the three hundred men who participated. In contrast, the most popular had well over a hundred. That's a big difference. Because even if there _is_ one man out there who loves every face, there's no guarantee he'll be the man you want.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/nana1.webp)

"I'm stuck with _you_?"

Women with both popular and unpopular faces do have a chance to catch the man of their dreams. And both may be considered equally pretty by someone. However, women with popular faces enjoy a more favorable [supply-demand ratio](../007-vi-market-forces/001-vi-market-forces.md) and a wider selection of mates. These advantages make them more likely to succeed. They're playing the same game, but fate has loaded the dice in their favor.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/cardinale43.jpg)

_Born lucky._

Of course, it's obvious to everyone that some women have more options than others. But there's a counterintuitive conclusion to draw here as well. Men are likely to end up with the _least popular_ facial type they select as one of their favorites. That's because they value this type more than other men, so they're willing to “pay” more for it than other men are. When they do, they'll still feel like they're getting a deal. And their unpopular lover will too. Everybody wins.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/marceau-jacket.jpg)

_There's a downside to popularity as well. Women with popular faces are “spoiled for choice.” Because they have so many suitors to choose from, they're subject to greater temptations, and easier matches already make their relationships less sticky than those of women whose faces have narrower appeal._

So it is advantageous to be one of the popular girls. But the real-world outcome isn't as unequal as you'd assume looking at the numbers in the chart. While your search for love will be more challenging, you can be unpopular with the crowd and still a dream come true to someone.

> _Fashionable Face Errors_
>
> While most fashion models do have good faces, the fashion world still has a tendency toward masculinization you should be wary of. Fashionable faces show a higher incidence of [rectangular structure](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/size/w1000/2023/09/tomb-raider-new.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [and](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/size/w1000/2021/09/vogue_model_crop2-1.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [hollow cheeks](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/ambro-crop2-2.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) than the [faces](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/01/head1-1.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [preferred](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/size/w1000/2023/09/ail3-1.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [by](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/07/monica2.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [men](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2021/11/anime.png?ref=jsanilac.com). Sometimes this masculine shape is [artificially emphasized on purpose](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/09/runway2024c.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com). Attractive female faces typically have a [rounded structure](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/01/rainsnowcrop.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), a modest jaw and chin, and a small nose with a narrow bridge, slightly upturned. Their cheeks are [full](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/06/conn3.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), and often form a [heart](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/10/ai-fantasy-crop-5.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [shape](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/08/dinner.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), whereas masculinized faces either [square this](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/model2.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) or [deform it toward a T](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/pejic-wedding-crop.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), as if sucking hard into a straw. Few men find that look beautiful, so you shouldn’t cultivate it intentionally.
