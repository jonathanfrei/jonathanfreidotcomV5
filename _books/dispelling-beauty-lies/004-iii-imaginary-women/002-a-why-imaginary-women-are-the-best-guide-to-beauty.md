> _Beauty Ideals Are Better Than Beauty Standards_
>
>  _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ aims to explain beauty ideals, not impose beauty standards. The difference is important. The term “beauty standards” implies women need to reach a certain standard to qualify as attractive, in the same way a lifeguard has to know how to swim and give CPR to be certified as a lifeguard. A beauty ideal is not a standard, because you don't need to reach it to be attractive. In fact, nobody's expected to reach the ideal at all. For example, an ideal lifeguard can hold her breath forever, swim miles without tiring, heal deep wounds, and fend off sharks with her bare hands. But you don't need to do these things to become a good lifeguard. Beauty ideals are useful because they give you a direction to aim for. And you can rest easy, because they don't imply any requirement that you reach them.
