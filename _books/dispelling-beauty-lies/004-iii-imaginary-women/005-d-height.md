Height is widely thought to have little effect on feminine beauty. The average sex doll is exactly as tall as the average woman worldwide, and the range of variation is close to that seen in the female population. This supports the conventional view.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/01/fashtall.jpg)

_Fashion models' height doesn't make them more beautiful, but it does ensure that average women's attempts to copy their look will be futile. You can't obtain a tall person's elongated figure by losing weight._

Nevertheless, height still causes confusion. Fashion models aren't just tall in absolute terms. They also have a low width-to-height ratio that makes them look reedy.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/09/prayingmantis.jpg)

_A praying mantis in a concrete dungeon (with handbags)._

Fashion marketing encourages women to believe these elongated proportions are the most beautiful. But that's not the case.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/01/3height11.jpg)

_Center: the original AI girl. Left: body shortened by 9%. Right: body lengthened by 9% (2SD of human height)._

Sex dolls, on the other hand, often do look elongated. Here material costs have intervened. Sex dolls are expensive, and [thicker dolls cost more](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/09/sex-doll-size-and-cost.png?ref=jsanilac.com). So poor men buy dolls with a low width-to-height ratio, short torso, and thin legs to save money without compromising other traits they care about more. You can see the result below. At left is an AI girl, and at right is a sex doll with cheap legs. Sex dolls are convenient examples for study because their measurements are provided by the manufacturer, but AI girls are more perfect examples of beauty because they're free of any financial constraints.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/09/stewardess.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/09/stewardesscrop.jpg)

_Business class or economy?_

This difference between sex dolls and other imaginary women is informative regardless of whether financial limitations are fully responsible. While men may prefer a particular width-to-height ratio, it's of secondary importance, and considerable deviation from the mean is tolerable. So elongated women aren't more beautiful than average women, but they're not particularly ugly either.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/01/height6.jpg)

_While the average man prefers the center figure, he isn't much bothered by variation between the left and right extremes._

> _Real-World Side-Effects of Height_
>
> All else being equal, taller women look less curvaceous than women of average height, whereas shorter women look more curvaceous. Because imaginary women are already quite curvaceous, shortening them can sometimes produce unusual extremes, and elongating them does little harm. But in the real world, where few have all the feminine curves men desire, this side-effect is more likely to work to the advantage of short women and the disadvantage of tall ones.
