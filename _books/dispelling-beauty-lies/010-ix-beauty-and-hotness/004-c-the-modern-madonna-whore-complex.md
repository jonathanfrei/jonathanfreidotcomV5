The misconceptions I've just identified are part of a broader error known as the Madonna-whore complex. The Madonna-whore complex is an irrational assumption that good character and high sex appeal can't coexist. In other words, that women can't be hot and virtuous at the same time. This supposed contradiction has no logical or factual basis.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/aig-bowl.jpg)

If food can be hot _and_ wholesome, [why can't women](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/marrypoll.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com)?

In past times the Madonna-whore complex was a product of religious guilt about sexuality. But while religious guilt has declined, the Madonna-whore complex hasn't. That's because the fashion industry and the modern media gave it a new form by associating high status with women who have limited or low sex appeal, and low status with women who have very high sex appeal.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/mandich.jpg)

_You might be afraid of looking “too” attractive. But the girls on your boyfriend's phone aren't. Possibly this is the wrong thing for you to be worrying about._

Contemporary culture claims to be sex positive, so women don't notice they've internalized an irrational complex. They just dismiss high sex appeal as objectionable or low class without thinking about it. The “rich women prefer small penises” argument I [refuted earlier](../005-iv-the-truth-about-breast-size/001-iv-the-truth-about-breast-size.md) is only one example among many. Misleading media messages have trained them to classify some of the most attractive behaviors, movements, styles, and body types as inherently trashy even when they aren't.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/turtleneck.jpg)

Purity of heart is to will many things. The idea that women need to be “dirty” to be sexy is responsible for some [hideous, stupid imagery](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/leathercat.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), and it's [not true at all](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/flat02.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com).

Some men are victims of a modern Madonna-whore complex too. Because pornography repeatedly shows them women of low character and high sex appeal, those most exposed to it may begin to associate sex appeal with low character. The stronger this association becomes in their minds, the harder it is for them to be satisfied in a relationship. Instead they crave one type of woman for sex, and a different type for marriage. If you catch a man separating hot and beautiful women into separate boxes that don't overlap, it's a red flag.

_One woman can never satisfy men who have a Madonna-whore complex. The safest thing you can do is avoid them._

The Madonna-whore complex is a damaging attitude you ought to shrug off. It forces you into an unnecessary conflict with the different sides of your nature that will prevent you from enjoying life as much as you could and realizing yourself as a whole and complete person. It keeps you on a leash, so you're only allowed to be “sort of hot” but never “really hot.” Can you think of a single good reason to stay on that leash?
