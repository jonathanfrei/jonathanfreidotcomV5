The modern Madonna-whore complex goes hand in hand with a sad mockery of morality I call puritanical voyeurism. Puritanical voyeurism is the view that normal women should follow unwritten rules that keep them dull and plain, while entertainers can be exciting and sexy and don't need to follow any rules at all.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/gray-paint-32-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/demi-horse-6-crop.jpg)

_Stop painting everything gray. Please._

Puritanical voyeurism is widespread today. Pornography and sexualized entertainment keep the public satisfied enough that they don't feel a need to improve their constricted real lives, and their constricted real lives leave them dissatisfied enough that they keep watching. So the puritanism enables the voyeurism, and vice versa.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/screen.jpg)

A happier society would be _more_ puritanical about the entertainment on their screens and _less_ puritanical about reality.

The real effect of puritanical voyeurism is to make normal women more boring and entertainers more exciting, so people would rather watch the latter than spend time with the former. That's not moral at all. In fact, it's _im_ moral.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/mudd-low.jpg)

_Why should the most powerful beauty and seduction techniques be reserved for the worst people?_

Genuine morality isn't about socially enforcing blandness and boredom while surfing smut on your phone. It's about supporting and improving loving relationships. And teaching normal women to look hotter and be more seductive does exactly that. That's why _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ occupies the true moral high ground, while moralizing puritans are, in actual practice, the ones encouraging turpitude.
