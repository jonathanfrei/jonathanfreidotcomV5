“Hot,” “beautiful,” “artistic,” and “interesting” are all distinct categories. In this section I've shown that hotness and beauty overlap and complement each other with no meaningful tradeoffs. For the other two categories, that's much less true.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/ernst-tanning-crop.jpg)

_Interesting._

Most people don't care about those other two categories, so for them the limited overlap is a non-issue. However, it can be confusing for women with a strong artistic sensibility. They correctly sense that artistically interesting looks don't always match the looks men find attractive, yet they aren't sure how to process this. If we take a moment to analyze what's going on here, we can easily explain it.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/aig-fish2.jpg)

_The artistic merit of AI girls is limited even in the best cases, but that's beside the point of the present article. They still teach us valuable lessons about beauty, hotness, and seduction._

The Venn diagram below shows four overlapping circles, one representing each category. As you can see, some looks are artistic, or at least interesting, yet neither beautiful nor even hot. This applies, for instance, to weird dresses, strange hairdos, unusual makeup, and women who are striking and memorable but visually unappealing in other respects. Photographers, painters, and designers love to explore these looks and find the region where they overlap with artistic merit. But that doesn't make them attractive.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/hb-venn-4.jpg)

A conceptual illustration, obviously not to scale. Typical inhabitants - Zone 1: already covered in this section with many examples. Zone 2: women with nice curves but plebian faces and a tacky sense of style. Zone 3: elegant women with weak curves and pretty faces, usually somewhat angular, who are slim and rather tall. Zone 4: art photography of unusual people, modernist portraiture and figure painting. Zone 5: traditional oil portraits of unremarkable women. Zone 6: [rare](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x_6Vlxoorvg&ref=jsanilac.com), and everyone will disagree over what qualifies. Zone 7: most first-rate artworks are interesting, beautiful, and artistic, but not hot, because it's very difficult to mix high hotness with contemplative moods.

The purple patch that's hot, beautiful, artistic, _and_ interesting is small. If you enjoy a challenge you can try to develop a personal look that squeezes into it. But be aware that this is a tall order you don't need to fill. Even talented artists struggle to create imagery that fits.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/bird.jpg)

_Rara avis._

The large majority of men are satisfied with women who look hot and beautiful but not “interesting” or “artistic,” and they'll choose them over women who are interesting and artistic but not hot or beautiful every time.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/birthday.jpg)

_Sacrificing hotness and beauty to look more artistic or interesting is usually a bad deal, because men care about the first two far more than the rest. This sacrifice can appeal to men with an arty minority taste when it's done well and in small doses. Push it too far and male attention will evaporate._

Looks that are artistic and interesting but not hot or beautiful can be worth contemplating. Just don't confuse this with attractiveness. In practice, they're best left on the canvas.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/rembrandt2.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/aig-steam-hot.jpg)

_Not hard to tell apart when you put it that way._

This same argument applies to fashion sense. Some women who stumble on this article have a well-developed fashion sense. They may even collect fashion photography. When they look at the imaginary women I present, they're inclined to turn up their noses, because the quality of the visual presentation and clothing doesn't satisfy their sensibilities.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/stare.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/redhead-hb.jpg)

_Not impressed? Glamour photography is intentionally stripped of atmospheric details in order to focus attention on the attractiveness of the models. You can't judge it by the same standard as fashion photography._

This reaction is understandable, but it misses the point. Fashionable, well styled, hot, and beautiful are different aesthetic categories, and _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ is only intended to teach you about the last two. While there's no reason they can't all overlap, the most instructive images for our purposes typically de-emphasize the other categories.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/aig-lace.jpg)

_One of the more fashionable AI girls._

To get the most out of this article, stop looking at women through the lens of fashion photography for a while. If you're enthused about styling and photography, that's fine. After you've thoroughly understood attractiveness, you'll have a new project ahead of you. Move past the limitations of the fashion world and find a way to have all the good things together. That's how it should have been in the first place, after all.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/th-thin.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/aig-carwash.jpg)

_You can have a great fashion sense while still making aesthetic errors as far as attractiveness goes. In fact, this is a common problem for girls who love fashion. And the reverse is true too. Mid-tier glamour models who score high in both hotness and beauty often have a mediocre fashion sense. Left: great style, but legs too thin. Right: who needs style?_
