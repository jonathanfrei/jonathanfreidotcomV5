Before we move on to the beauty tips section of this article I'm going to address a difficult problem. How to break it to her? You can skip the next few paragraphs if this question isn't relevant to you.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/demi-phone.jpg)

A man who wants to share _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ with a female acquaintance in a non-confrontational way should try telling her, “This gives a pretty accurate description of what most men like.” You don't need to say anything about whether it does or doesn't represent your own tastes. However, if you’re thinking about sharing it with your wife or girlfriend, you're on more dangerous ground.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/FiEWWZZWYAE08Qm-1.jpg)

_A woman with naturally great curves is more likely to respond well to this article._

If she has a strong mind and you have excellent communication, you might be able to send her a direct link or even read _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ together. Sadly, that's a very rare combination. Most women today simply cannot accept the truth about feminine beauty, and will react with unreasonable dismissals regardless of how you present it. This is a consequence of the psychological block described in the [previous section](../011-x-correcting-homework/001-x-correcting-homework.md), reinforced by damaging media messages.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/russia4.jpg)

_How brave are you?_

To introduce sensitive women to the ideas in _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ , I suggest you first direct them to one of the several companion articles instead, depending on their interests. “[The Illusion of Dominance](https://www.jsanilac.com/dominance/)” introduces _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ backward, starting with the section on seduction. “[101 Ways to Be Prettier, Sexier, and More Seductive](https://www.jsanilac.com/101/)” presents the advice in list form, starting with the least offensive. And “[Ultrahumanism](https://www.jsanilac.com/ultrahumanism/)” offers a near-future vision of a world where we improve human beauty and health instead of allowing AI to dominate. If none of these suit, you can always link her to the section on [_masculine_ beauty](../009-viii-a-note-on-masculine-beauty/001-viii-a-note-on-masculine-beauty.md) and ask if she agrees.

 _Made of rainbows._

Some readers are keen to share _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ with their daughters, but unsure about the right age. When I discussed this question with parents I found two schools of thought. One is that it's best to wait until her late teens, because so much of the article is “adult content.” Furthermore, young girls may not yet have the intellectual ability to understand all the writing, which could lead to damaging misinterpretations.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/01/phone3.jpg)

_Unfortunately the worst thing you can imagine has already played on her phone._

However, other parents make reasonable arguments against this delay, and believe the early teens are best. They point out that kids are already exposed to extreme pornography through their phones (typically around age twelve), and exposed to false media messages about beauty and sexuality even earlier, some of which are very harmful. Pretending this isn't happening and “protecting” them from true information can't be helpful.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/cardinale-spin.jpg)

_If you discover what teens are already up to on their phones when you’re not watching, you might change your mind about waiting._

Learning the truth at a young age may also be less challenging to their self-esteem, because the fullness of their womanhood is something off in the misty future. This makes it easier for them to assimilate the information in an unprejudiced and unoffended way, so it’s ready at hand when they finally need it.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/Las_majas.jpg)

_Scheduling a guided tour might make more sense than leaving her education up to “whatever she happens to run into on the internet.”_

Furthermore, by the age of seventeen, kids often ignore even the best parental advice, bizarrely trusting media that strangers have designed to manipulate them over wisdom their parents have gleaned from long experience.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/01/phone4sand.png)

I do think it's safest to share the facts with her in the controlled home environment before she leaves for college, but beyond that I won't take one side or the other. It's your responsibility as a parent to make the best choice you can. A compromise position is to show her only [particular excerpts](../013-xii-reality-based-beauty-tips/002-1-finding-the-best-weight-for-you.md) you've selected. This makes sense if she's still quite young but already being harmed by beauty lies promoting excessive thinness.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/thoughtfulanime-1.jpg)

When I polled readers about the best age to encounter _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ for the first time, answers ranged from twelve to nineteen, with sixteen being the most popular answer.

Women will find it interesting to know I've had significantly more men write me that they've shared _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ with their daughters than with their wives or girlfriends. This is the best endorsement anyone could give, though also a little sad, because life is short, and we need opportunities to enjoy beauty now and not just in future generations.
