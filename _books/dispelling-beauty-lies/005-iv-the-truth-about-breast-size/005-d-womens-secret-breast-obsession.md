It isn't surprising that men are fascinated by large breasts. But the public response to the data I've just presented reveals that many women are too.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/ai-maid.jpg)

_Another maid who'll keep your husband faithful._

Women react much more strongly to remarks on breast size than any other beauty topic, including weight and age. This implies they subconsciously believe, or at least fear, that large breasts are the centerpiece of feminine sex appeal. The most impassioned denials come from the most breast obsessed. After all, if they didn't think breast size was extremely important, they wouldn't be so upset. Some insist on pretending size doesn't matter even as they jealously bully the best-endowed women.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/11/tubareadscrop2-3.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/11/stripe-crop2.jpg)

Some women are so breast obsessed that they've convinced themselves my [entire website](https://www.jsanilac.com/) is devoted to the topic of breast size, and say so to anyone foolish enough to listen to them. In fact, this section constitutes less than 3% of the article you're currently reading, and less than 1% of the writing on my site. Anyone who can read a wide-ranging 100,000-word article on beauty and seduction and believe it's all about breasts is suffering from a severe case of obsession, and should seek help.

In the world of breast-size denialism, the view count of the video below is completely inexplicable and mystifying. Back here in reality, the cause is dead obvious.

The almost universal refusal to publicly acknowledge just how big a contribution big breasts make to feminine beauty is one of the most bizarre social phenomena of our time. Future generations will surely wonder what the fuss was about, why so many people failed at basic pattern recognition, and whether this section really had to be so thorough. Unfortunately, it did.

Because of women's secret breast obsession, when it becomes impossible to deny the facts about size they sometimes leap all the way from blind faith in the conventional lie that “more than a handful is too much” to the opposite extreme. They may worry it's impossible for them to be attractive to men if they can't match up to the model below, and give up. Needless to say, that isn't a balanced response.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/knit-bikini.jpg)

_Rare, actually._

Beauty is a composite of _many_ elements, and breast size is only one of them. If an oversimplified numerical estimate will ease your mind, you can think of it as counting for one or two points on a ten-point “beauty scale.” That's significant. But it still means almost all the points lie elsewhere. And in real life, seductive skill matters too.

> _Looks Matter, Letters Don't_
>
> Letters and numbers are a convenient way to organize data about breast size, but they're not as meaningful as they sound. The volume of each cup size varies with the band, the brand, and the country. And the same volume looks much different on [women with different body sizes](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/implant-height.png?ref=jsanilac.com). Where attractiveness is concerned, the visual impression of size is what actually matters. So don't worry about cup letters. In fact, you're better off forgetting them. Rely instead on the images presented in this article.
