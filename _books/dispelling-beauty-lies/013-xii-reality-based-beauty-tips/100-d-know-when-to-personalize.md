_The basic rules for clothing are consistent, but that doesn't mean every attractive style suits your personality._

However, some elements of attractiveness _do_ depend on personality. This is true of seduction technique and motion, and also stylistic elements of your clothing, accessories, and hairstyles. You need to find a sweet spot where you move, behave, and dress attractively in a way that still feels like you. Rote imitation of the fashions worn by AI girls, for example, may go quite wrong if they don't suit you. The general principles will still hold, but the specifics need to be tweaked to personalize them. Don't forget to take this into account.
