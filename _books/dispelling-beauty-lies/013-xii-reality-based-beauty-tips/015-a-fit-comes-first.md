Because a small waist is such a critical element of feminine beauty, you should always wear clothes that are fitted at the waist, not loose. This is the single most important principle pertaining to clothing, and in past times all women knew to follow it. However, at present most of the population is [overweight](./002-1-finding-the-best-weight-for-you.md), so typical clothes on the rack are made to suit an unpleasantly bulbous waist. Even for healthy women with exactly average curves, they often won't fit properly. And the problem is much greater for women with the coveted thick hourglass shown below.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2021/06/monarchiste.jpg)

_Women who have this attractive body type need to take special care in their clothing selection. It's the most difficult to dress in modern clothes, and also suffers the most from poor fit and bad posture, which can conceal its natural advantages and cause it to look dumpy._

Stretch fabrics or elastic bands are an easy shortcut solution, but sometimes a tailor is necessary. Don't skimp. It's well worth the money to have everything altered to match your personal measurements. In fact, wearing clothes off the rack without having them tailored is the most common reason curvaceous women fail to benefit from their gifts.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/08/size-guide.png)

_A sizing guide. None of the standard clothing sizes will fit a 24" waist and 40" hips at the same time. For a small fee, a tailor can save you from looking like a blob. If you're perplexed as to why you draw so little advantage from great curves, ill-fitted clothing is almost certainly the answer._

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/08/handbag.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/08/pink-2.jpg)

_Left: this woman will definitely require a tailor for any clothing that doesn't stretch. Right: fairytale costume dresses, like most traditional clothing, are well-suited to the hourglass figure._

There are a few exceptions to the rule. Very straight and thin women can do well with flowing clothes, and top-heavy women with a relatively thick middle may look best in an empire waist. Women with any body type can of course wear baggy clothes as a _sheer_ outer layer, provided an underlayer that hugs their figure is clearly visible.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/06/this.jpg)

_Flared skirts with a fitted waist can help women who have narrow hips create the impression of an hourglass figure._

If you’re socially anxious and wear baggy or poorly fitted clothes to hide your body even though you have a healthy weight, please stop. There are better ways to be modest. Wear snug but high-necked shirts, and long skirts that are tightly fitted at the waist. These look modest but still allow people to notice that you have an attractive figure.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2021/04/eugene-joors-demonstration-of-turtleneck-effect-1.jpg)

_Well-fitted clothes can be modest without looking sloppy._
