Quantity and quality aren't the only troublesome sexual differences. You may be disturbed to know that a large majority of men have fantasies involving multiple partners that don't go away when they're in a relationship. In fact, they usually get stronger the longer a relationship lasts.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/smallbutt-236-1.jpg)

At first glance men's desire for variety seems to create an insuperable problem. But once again, there's a simple solution. You can _simulate_ variety and multiplicity by making significant changes to your clothing, makeup, hair, and comportment. Gradual, minor alterations will have little effect, because the purpose is to look like a “new” woman.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/Le_Chevalier_aux_Fleurs_2560x1600.png)

_Harem fantasies are impossible for one woman to fulfill, but possible for one woman to game. How much a style refresh will help depends on your lover's tastes and sensibilities._

You should do this on two different time scales. First, consider a style refresh every couple of years. Second, dramatically change your style for a day, or a few hours, when you're in the mood. You don't need to do this constantly, but after you've been together for a while you ought to do it occasionally, and more often if you find it works well.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/demi-glasses-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/demisnow-1.jpg)

_Successful glamour models don't stick to one look. They vary their styles to keep their audience interested. You can do the same thing._

Men's taste for variety is strong enough that even cutting your hair short, one of the worst beauty mistakes you can make, will briefly increase your lover's interest. Fortunately there are innumerable better ways to vary your style that make seduction easier in a lasting relationship.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/yuka-kuramochi-2-crop.jpg)

Costumes and costume accessories are particularly effective for one-day style changes. Behaving a little in character can add to the appeal, though you don't need to engage in roleplay if you don't want to. For instance, a wig in a different color can already be enough to create the illusion of variety. An exotic article of clothing that you'd never normally wear will help.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/vitti-harem2-1.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/gene2-1.jpg)

Animal ears are an especially valuable accessory. They're a very small change in reality, but they cause men's instincts to register you as a different person. Where full costumes are concerned, French-maid outfits and schoolgirl uniforms with pleated plaid miniskirts are the two most reliably attractive.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/oc-ears.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/01/poll4crop1.jpg)

_Yes, you really do win points with men just for playing dress-up. Nobody said life always has to be hard._

> _The Variety Rule_
>
> Because men need variety to sustain sexual interest, you should use styles, costumes, accessories, and comportment to create the illusion that you're multiple women. (It's also fun.)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/cardinale-10.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/cardinale9-3.jpg)

_Both sides now._

> _Escalate_
>
> It's unwise to apply all the seduction techniques in _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ in one go from the start of a new relationship. Instead you should begin more tentatively and phase them in gradually over months. Escalating over time lets excitement build as your relationship deepens, and it naturally creates a variety effect that keeps men more interested. Too much too soon also risks awkwardness. This is especially true for virgins, who should hold off on using most seduction techniques until they have some experience.
