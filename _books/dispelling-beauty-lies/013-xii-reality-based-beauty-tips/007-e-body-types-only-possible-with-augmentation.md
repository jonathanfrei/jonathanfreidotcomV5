Some body types you see in the media are only possible with cosmetic surgery. The “skinny-hot” type, shown below, is one of these. It requires not just a rare natural body type, but an exceptionally well-executed large breast augmentation as well. Skinny-hot isn't quite as popular with men as thicker types, and women tend to be more impressed by it than they should be.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/03/honey-large2-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/03/honey3-crop.jpg)

_The skinny-hot body type features a very narrow frame that resembles a teenager of fifteen or so, with modest hips, a tiny waist, a small but pert rear, and large breasts that would never accompany such a figure without surgery. I've chosen a cartoon example to remind you it's not a natural look._

You might assume you can emulate this body type simply by losing weight. That won't work, even setting aside the issue of implants. The skinny-hot type isn't really about low bodyfat levels. It's about having an unusually narrow frame. Examine the abdominal area in the images above and below. No muscle or bone are visible, but only soft flesh, including a little fat. This is essential to get it right, and few can.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/beach.jpg)

_Fat is evident at the hips despite this model's very narrow frame._

Women who try to chase a skinny-hot body when it doesn't suit them will end up looking like a [pile of sticks and stones](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/doe.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) with [hollow cheeks](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/ambro-crop2-2.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com). That's not the look at all. So if you have any doubts, don't try.

_The only women who should consider pursuing the skinny-hot look are those who naturally have a narrow frame at a healthy weight and are happy to buy implants._

Below you can see an attempt at the skinny-hot look that doesn't quite hit the mark. The model has too little bodyfat in these photos, which causes her abs to be excessively defined. By consequence, she lacks the feminine softness of the examples just shown. Instead her beauty is hampered by an undercurrent of masculinity. Her implants might distract from this flaw, but they don't fix it. If you're determined to emulate the skinny-hot look, you need to keep your weight dialed in to the narrow range where your midsection looks very slim but also soft.

It's now common for glamour models to use fat transfer from the waist to the hips, butt, and thighs, creating a voluptuous hourglass that's hard or impossible to obtain naturally. The example below goes too far, and I don't recommend you imitate it. I'm including it here because it demonstrates the effects very clearly. Women who use the same procedure with more moderation have good results.

_Unlikely to be natural._

Be aware that today's top glamour models have _all_ had cosmetic surgery of one kind or another, whatever they may claim in public. This includes not only models with the plainly artificial body types I've just cited, but also models with seemingly natural body types.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/demi-horse-3.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/darkness.jpg)

_Does this model normally ride horses barefoot on the beach, or are these photos staged?_

On the best models these surgeries may not be obvious. But that doesn't mean they didn't happen. So if you're wondering why you aren't able to duplicate the appearance of your favorite inspirational photos, stop wondering. Most likely they're not targets anyone should expect to reach unaided.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/beach-shower.jpg)

_This and the previous aren't inherently unnatural physiques. But you're still exceedingly unlikely to get them with natural means alone._

Fortunately, the advice in this article makes it easy to improve your beauty and seductive power with or without a surgeon's help. I'll discuss cosmetic surgery in detail later and you can decide whether it's right for you. But even if you're sure you do want it someday, your best natural look should always come first.
