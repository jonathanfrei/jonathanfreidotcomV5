It's tempting to guess after the cause of men's narrow tastes, but for our purposes here we'll just stick to the facts. Men find women more attractive when they're dressed in a standard palette consisting of white, black, red, and pink, as well as denim blue.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/fuyu-kukan-crop.jpg)

Blue is good for jeans, but not great elsewhere. And beyond jeans, baby blue (a flower color) is notably more feminine than navy blue (not a flower color).

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/marceau9-1.jpg)

_Also a trendy color for oceans._

To compare men's tastes to women's typical clothing choices, I counted the colors of over two hundred women in casual dress on a street corner in Gangnam and a boardwalk in Florida. Women in both places tend to wear too much beige, tan, gray, and blue, and not enough red and white. I also observed women passing through an airport terminal in Texas and saw even more egregious color failures there, with both olive green and gray markedly more common than pink, and blue tops worn far too often as well.

The few women who do wear red usually wear the wrong red, not the rich, ruby red men like most.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/06/demi24.jpg)

_Not all reds are created equal. Ruby red is the most powerful, and several other rich reds work too. But primary red can be questionable, and orange-tinted reds don't hit the spot. All the reds in this photo are good._

Today other beauty writers promote an elaborate system of color "seasons." Unfortunately their system isn't evidence based, and the advice it produces is often poor. For instance, the seasons system tells women with a "true autumn" complexion to wear browns, oranges, and related neutrals, but these are among the least attractive colors.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/06/castan3.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/sweeping2-1.jpg)

_If you don't trust evidence derived from AI girls as much as popular theories about "color seasons," just ask yourself whether a man and a woman with the exact same skin tone look equally attractive in hot pink. The answer is obvious, but how to explain it with color seasons and complexion is not._

It's true that complexion, hair color, and eye color can influence which shades flatter you. But the inherent appeal of the most-loved clothing colors is independent from this influence. Ignoring that fact is a mistake.

Our observations point us toward a few simple guidelines with universal value. First and foremost, avoid flesh tones, or colors that are close to flesh tones. That includes beige, tan, taupe, brown, and peach too. Though not quite as bad, you should also avoid army greens, which are greens mixed with tan or brown. Olive is the most common example of a bad green. I call this collection of flesh and camo tones the unlucky palette.

_The U.S. Military has produced a useful guide to the worst clothing colors for women. It's called "camo." In the rare occasions when AI girls or glamour models do appear in these unattractive colors it's for the sake of contrast or storytelling rather than beauty per se._

Beiges and tans are unique in that they're okay for bottoms, uninspiring for tops and dresses, and horrible for lingerie. Once in a while a beige dress can be justified by the soothing effect it creates, but this is something normally best reserved for walls.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/aig-beige-bottoms.jpg)

_Beige clothes and bobs are overrepresented in this article compared to their real value, because I've included a fair number of AI girls in the intentionally bland “everyday” style._

Second, prioritize the standard palette of white, black, denim, pink, and red in your wardrobe.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/01/boug-pink.jpg)

_“Women are flowers” isn't a perfect rule. Yellow, peach, and orange are floral but not worth wearing frequently, and emerald is a decent non-floral color, at least for redheads. Nevertheless, thinking of men as sturdy trees and women as delicate flowers is so often a helpful mindset for understanding beauty and seduction that I'm writing the metaphor here anyway._

> _Women Are Flowers_
>
> The best colors for women are flower colors, whereas colors that don't appear in flowers look better on men.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/camel.jpg)

This model is about to _turn_ red, which is not advisable.

Red is too loud to wear all the time, but you should still consider wearing it more often than you probably do. You don't need to wear a red dress. Accents can help too.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/06/young.jpg)

_On point. You can wear pink more frequently than red because it's less aggressive._

Third, wear a color from the “variety” palette occasionally. This palette consists of dark blue, emerald, purple, gold, orange, turquoise, teal, and variations on these colors. Once every week or two is probably good.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/06/ai-purple-planoe-1.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/06/demi-lemon-2-1.jpg)

_Yellow and faded oranges are rather unpopular with men, but not strictly off-limits. In this successful photo shoot yellow was chosen to match the fruit. Purple and gold are the most underexploited variety colors today._

The variety palette is less attractive than the standard palette. However, if you use it sparingly, it has the advantage of novelty. For example, if you usually wear black and white, when you show up in violet it will draw interest and seem exciting. Redheads benefit more from unusual colors, and should use them a little more often.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/06/tuba-63-1.jpg)

_You can stand out by wearing a particular signature color from the variety palette much more frequently than I've recommended. But the distinctiveness this grants you is likely to come at the expense of attractiveness. For most women that's not a great deal._

Neutrals obviously draw less attention than other colors. And women default to neutral bottoms and colorful tops. However, don’t neglect the reverse option. Combining neutral tops with colorful bottoms can create more excitement. This effect applies to miniskirts and shorts above all.

_Turn the beat around. Right is more exciting than left._
