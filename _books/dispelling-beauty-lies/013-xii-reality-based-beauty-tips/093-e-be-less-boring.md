It's a sad fact that most women today are boring, and certainly more boring than they were in the previous century. They spend their leisure hours watching video clips of cute cats, lifestyle influencers buying groceries, and celebrities gossiping about other celebrities. They have no real interests, or even hobbies. To be clear, most men today are boring too, but this article isn't addressed to them.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/aig-chess3.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/aig-pool3-crop-1.jpg)

Men have low expectations for women, so if you're even just _okay_ at something, they'll be impressed.

Being boring won't make you less beautiful or worse in bed. But it _will_ make you less interesting to the opposite sex. And if we're honest, it's probably not the most rewarding way to spend your time.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/marilyn-archery.png)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/cardinale-guitar.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/f1c28f315305f4a682711eda4e03fe4d.jpg)

Developing an interest or skill in any domain at all can help you stand out and seem more unique and exciting. It gives you opportunities to meet people and start conversations. You don't even need to be especially knowledgeable or skilled.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/aig-archer-1-1.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/aig-camera2.jpg)

_Obviously more interesting than the exact same woman sitting there looking at her phone._

For instance, if you're interested in archery or flamenco music you'll become “that girl who's into archery” or “that girl who loves flamenco” instead of just “that girl who's basically like all the other girls.” It doesn't matter if you're fantastic at archery or a true expert in flamenco. And while being interested in male-dominated topics might stand out the most, being interested in fashion or flower arrangement is a plus too.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/demi-reading.jpg)

_Ever heard of reading books? They come in all sorts of varieties and can make you knowledgeable about any topic._

So for your romantic prospects and for your own sake, try being less boring. Don't be a zombie. Get interested in something. Anything. No, being less boring it isn't as important as looking hot. But whether or not it catches you the man of your dreams, you won't regret it.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/aig-guitar-2.jpg)

_Getting interested in music has social and personal benefits even if you don't play an instrument._
