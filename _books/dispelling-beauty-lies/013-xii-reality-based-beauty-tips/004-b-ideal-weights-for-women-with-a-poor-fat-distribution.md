The optimal weight for attractiveness depends on your personal bodyfat distribution, not an arbitrary number we can predetermine. The better your bodyfat distribution, the more you benefit from a few extra pounds. The worse your bodyfat distribution, the more important it is to stay slim. To clarify I'll go over a series of examples, starting with the thinnest.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/bmi25ccrop.jpg)

_These two women have the same BMI, but the left is attractive at this weight, while the right is not. It's important not to exceed the weight supported by your body type._

The top fashion model below looks both too thin and too masculine, with no feminine softness at all. A [visibly muscular abdomen](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/01/confidentbad3.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) is unattractive to most men. This is certainly not the best weight for her body. Even if you don't carry weight well, you shouldn't copy her physique.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/doe.jpg)

_Not advisable regardless of your body type._

If you have a poor bodyfat distribution (i.e. fat goes to your belly more than your hips, chest, rear, or thighs), adding weight will do more harm than good, and staying rather thin is indeed the best choice. For instance, the model below isn't fat, but because the fat deposits she does have are concentrating in the belly area, she needs to maintain a low weight. While you don't need a flat belly, it's still important to avoid a bulbous, protruding one, and especially so if you lack curves elsewhere.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/arobbie.jpg)

Don't judge your ideal weight by your arm circumference. “Thin arms, thick thighs” is indeed the best combination. However, arms are relatively unimportant. If only your arms are too thick, ignore them and aim for an attractive weight elsewhere.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/harriet2--2--2.jpg)

_Thin arms are usually a plus, but only to a certain point. This example is rather too thin, and any thinner would definitely be worse._

No matter how poor your bodyfat distribution, you shouldn't overdo weight loss. Men who prefer thin women still dislike skeletons. The model below has a thin but healthy and attractive figure. You should never aim to be thinner than this.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/ninabeachcrop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/nina9crop.jpg)

_Don't go below this weight regardless of your body type._
