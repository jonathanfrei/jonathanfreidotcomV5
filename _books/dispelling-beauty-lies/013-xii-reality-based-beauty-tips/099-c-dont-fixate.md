Fixation creates the most confusion where the nose is concerned. If you repeat the same word enough times in a row, it will start to sound weird. If you stare at your nose too long, a similar thing will happen. It will start to look weird too, even if there's nothing wrong with it. This doesn't just apply to the nose. When you fixate on a feature (any feature), your perceptions become untrustworthy. If you find yourself exhibiting OCD-like behavior, your self-perception is unlikely to be accurate and balanced. Take a step back and stop worrying for a while.

_Anxiety makes your self image less accurate. Don't try to evaluate your appearance when you're feeling anxious._

It's common for women to obsess over minor elements of beautification like painting their nails while neglecting essential basics like clothing fit or weight. The previous section is a good list of fundamentals. Don't bother worrying about details until you've gotten these fundamentals right to the extent possible.
