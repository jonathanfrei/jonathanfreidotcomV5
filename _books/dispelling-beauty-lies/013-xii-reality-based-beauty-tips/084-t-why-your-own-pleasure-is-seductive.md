We've previously seen that men like signs of female desire and pleasure, and that while women can exaggerate these for effect, they still need to be [authentic](./074-j-the-jargon-of-authenticity.md) at root. When we consider both points together, the obvious implication is that women's pleasure makes an important contribution to men's. In other words, to seduce well, you need to enjoy yourself. If your priority is to please him as much as possible, enjoying yourself amounts to an obligation.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/betty-howard-1.jpg)

_Submission leads back to pleasure. That's one reason there's no need to worry about, let alone prioritize, equality._

Men don't have an equivalent to _Dispelling Beauty Lies_. And contrary to what seduction gurus tell them, women are all different, and men are poor mind-readers. Thus, the most important thing you can do to enjoy yourself more is to figure out what you like best through fantasizing and experimenting, and communicate it to your lover in clear words. Be specific. Waiting and hoping for him to understand without being told, or expecting him to take subtle hints, is a mistake that could leave you waiting forever.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/blonde-black.jpg)

_You might think the hints of pleasure in your voice and expression suffice, but it often makes a big difference to verbally explain what you want and what you're enjoying, so there's no room for doubt._

I surveyed men on their response to women's requests. Even when women request something most men don't like, they almost always agree to do it. Of course, you're probably reluctant to request things your partner wouldn't like, whether he'll do them or not. But only excessive displays of violent domination fall into this category with any frequency. Men will be happy to give you anything else you'll enjoy, and they'll appreciate the clarity of your verbal requests. In fact, they'll find the requests themselves exciting, especially if you present them as begging. Thus we can formulate one more rule of seduction.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/11/aig-thigh-6-1.jpg)

_Men are famous for not being able to take subtle hints when you want them to, and women are famous for expecting them to do it anyway. Be brave and break that cycle._

> _The Communication Rule_
>
> Communicate your desires clearly and directly so you get what you need to enjoy yourself. Begging works especially well. Then communicate your enjoyment when he gets it right.

After we accept reality and dissect it in the clear light of day, we discover the perennial problems that plague relationships are tractable. By giving pleasure regularly and casually you can satisfy men's need for quantity. By changing your style to seem like multiple women, you can satisfy their need for variety. By staging domination in the right way, you can make it enjoyable for them too. By communicating your needs clearly, you can share the excitement of your own pleasure. And by using all the seduction strategies I've described together, you'll overcome natural incompatibilities and strengthen your relationship. In return, you can expect the quality attention you enjoy the most.
