The belly area is perhaps the most confusing of all. Many women struggle to correctly envision how their belly ought to look. Some overweight women think nothing's wrong, while other women who are already too thin convince themselves they need to become even thinner.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/lev-tchistoksky-1.jpg)

_The belly area in this example is perfectly fine, but more would quickly be too much._

A woman's belly is not supposed to be flat. It's normal for it to bump out a little. But it's not supposed to stick out excessively either. [This AI girl](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/08/aig323.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) and [this model](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/rain41.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) show the approximate maximum attractive belly protrusion even in a voluptuous figure with a fortunate fat distribution. The majority of women should aim for less. How much less depends on your overall figure, not the way your belly looks in isolation. To get this right, study the “[Finding The Best Weight For You](./002-1-finding-the-best-weight-for-you.md)” section carefully. Save the examples that are closest to your own body type and compare them to photos of yourself taken at a ten-foot distance. You can also use examples from elsewhere in this article as a reference point.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/ai-think.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/aig-stockings-crop.jpg)

_Both of these AI girls have a well-balanced belly._

Some lucky women look good at a wide range of weights, but for most women there will be one best weight. If you're in the second category, determining what this weight is and staying reasonably close to it should be your number-one beauty priority. And this particularly applies to getting the belly area right. If your butt or chest look great but your belly is protruding well beyond the comparable examples in this article, you still need to lose weight.
