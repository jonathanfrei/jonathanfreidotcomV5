At present Motiva implants are the safest and best. The alternatives entail a higher risk of capsular contracture that isn't worth the savings. The remaining choice is between Motiva Rounds and Motiva Ergonomix. Ergonomix look extremely realistic, while Rounds look fuller and more youthful, but sometimes a bit fake. If you have little natural breast tissue and little bodyfat, Ergonomix is normally the best choice, because the artificiality of Rounds will be apparent on your body. If you have some natural breast tissue and bodyfat to smooth out the edges, either can work depending on the look you want. Your surgeon will help you make this decision at your consultation.

> _Age And Breast Size Revisited_
>
> Due to the interplay between curves and neoteny, breast size has a smaller impact on the attractiveness of younger women. This seems to change the most between eighteen and twenty. Furthermore, natural breast development may not be complete until eighteen, and in some cases even later. So teenage girls should be advised not to worry about their chest size at all, let alone rush into an augmentation.
