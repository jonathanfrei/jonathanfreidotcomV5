Stripes aren't especially common on imaginary women, and wide vertical stripes like those shown above right are extremely rare. That said, glamour models sometimes wear narrower horizontal stripes without the sky falling in.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/mudd-icecream-2.jpg)

_Unspectacular, but not a disaster._

Animal print is even rarer on imaginary women, though again glamour models wear it fairly often. In this case, however, there's good reason to believe glamour models are overdoing it.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/demi-finger.webp)

_A great photo, but still not a style you should wear often._

In theory, animal print is supposed to represent your wild and sexy side. In practice, it's just not as sexy as it sounds. There's no rule that you can't wear animal print. But it's rarely a good idea.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/aig-pink-leopard-animal-print.jpg)

_A rare AI girl in animal print. There were zero instances of animal print in the sample of over four hundred AI girls I used for my color analysis. If men were impressed by the jungle-beast look, they'd depict it more often._

Floral patterns occur reasonably often in images of imaginary women. They're soft, feminine, sunny, and springlike. These are all normally good characteristics. However, I have a lingering suspicion that floral is a bit less compelling at night, and can't quite hit the top range of sex appeal. I wouldn't want to exaggerate, because it's tough to prove and could be wrong. But on nights when you're aiming for maximum impact, you might want to consider other options first. For a picnic, floral's a great idea.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/aig-floral-4.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/jetsun-13.webp)

Gingham and plaid are the patterns that boost sex appeal the most, provided they're used correctly. Plaid is mainly for minis, and can work for other skirts as well. Elsewhere, however, it rarely impresses.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/plaid-mini-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/aigirl-plaid-green.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/khosa-plaid-1.jpg)

_Plaid skirts are amenable to an expanded color range including blues and greens._

Gingham is great for tops, skirts, dresses, and lingerie, provided the cut is good. Gingham clothes should be rather skimpy. With overly voluminous clothing, the sexy effect disappears, or even reverses.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/gingham-3.webp)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/demi-yellow-gingham.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/gingham-pink.webp)

_The waist of the dress at right should really be a bit lower and more snug._

We've seen earlier that juvenile print has high sex appeal. However, this seems to apply uniquely to panties and pajamas. Polka dots can have a pleasant effect too, though their impact is much weaker than plaid, gingham, and animal print. As for patterns not mentioned above, they occur so rarely that it's hard to say much. Just use good judgment, and be cautious about high-contrast geometric patterns with wide elements, because these tend to distract from feminine beauty.
