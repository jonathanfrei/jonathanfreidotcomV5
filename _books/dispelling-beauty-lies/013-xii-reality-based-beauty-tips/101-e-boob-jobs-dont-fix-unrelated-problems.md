A boob job can increase your attractiveness significantly, but it should be the “cherry on top,” not the main course. It doesn't solve other beauty problems. While it might change your ideal weight slightly, it doesn't compensate for being meaningfully over- or underweight, and it doesn't improve your style or your makeup or anything else. Make sure you cover all the other fundamentals _before_ your surgery.
