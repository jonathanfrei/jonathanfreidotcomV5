Classic paintings almost never depict beautiful women with a tan. Women once wore broad-brimmed hats and carried [parasols](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/media/2023/08/France-1902-crop.mp4?ref=jsanilac.com) to avoid them. Reader selections for [best breast and butt shape](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/11/bestshapeinfographic.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) are either untanned or lightly tanned, and AI girls and [popular anime girls](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/12/popular.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) normally have no tan at all.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/03/ai-blazr-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/03/ai-pale.png)

_2D girls usually have luminous skin that's paler than human glamour models'._

Below you'll find a composite image of skin patches from over a hundred randomly selected AI girls that were posted to the #aigirl hashtag on social media. All patches were clipped from the center of the chest, the typical focal point of AI-girl images, which provides the clearest and most consistent representation of coloration. The many patches with a grayish tint are dimly lit, not tanned.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/10/aigirlcolor.jpg)

_The skin tones of AI girls give us a rough idea of the coloration men find most attractive, and they're remarkably consistent. AI girls depicted in shadowy lighting were excluded from this sample._

You can see at a glance that untanned skin is far more common than tanned skin. In fact, fewer than ten percent of the images are distinctly tanned. Furthermore, around half are notably paler and brighter than human women in normal everyday lighting, suggesting that unnaturally luminous skin is particularly beautiful.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/torso2h.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/torso2k.webp)

_Generic sex-doll torsos are a good indicator of what the largest number of men find attractive._

However, [uncensored](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/08/yahoo.png?ref=jsanilac.com) [search](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/08/googleuncensored.png?ref=jsanilac.com) [results](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/01/searchresultstop50.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [include](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/08/binguncensored.png?ref=jsanilac.com) [both](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/10/uncens_searchresults.png?ref=jsanilac.com) untanned and tanned skin in fairly equal proportions. And while the generic sex-doll torsos [discussed earlier](../001-dispelling-beauty-lies.md) have untanned or lightly tanned skin, untanned skin is only modestly more popular than tanned skin in the complete set of dolls.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/Geraldine_163CM_F_DC57_011.webp)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/doll-skin-tone.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/FWD092-18.webp)

_Skin tones of sex dolls at an American retailer: 56% white, 37% tanned, 5% brown, 1% black, 0.3% other._

The data is thus mixed, though the evidence against tanning is stronger than the evidence in its favor. Tanned skin appears to serve as a fashionable signal whose intrinsic effect on feminine beauty is neutral at best, and likely negative. At the very least, its value is overestimated by women today.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/08/Biblis.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/aig-turquoise.jpg)

_Historically, tanning was strongly dispreferred. Figures from classic paintings almost universally match the luminous skin tones of typical AI girls._

You can decide whether and how much to tan based on local conditions and fashions, as well as your personal style. But despite its popularity, there's no basis to recommend sunbathing for aesthetic purposes.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/01/beforeafterai2-1.png)

_Percent of men who rated these images "very attractive" (n=1,016). Left and center are the same person, right is a lookalike AI girl. Makeup and lighting that unnaturally enhance luminosity should provide a beauty advantage._

Since excessive sun exposure accelerates aging and tanning is unlikely to have a significant positive effect on your appearance, the default choice should be to avoid exposing your skin to more sun than is necessary for good health.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/avril-lund.jpg)

_A good photo of a bad idea._

Heavy tan lines from sunbathing in bikinis have an especially unfortunate effect, and are best avoided.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/doll-rear-12.webp)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/09/doll-rear-12b.webp)

_Imaginary women don't have tan lines._

If you're very pale and have no tan at all, don't let anyone tell you it makes your skin looks plain and sickly. This view is not supported by contemporary evidence, and it's strongly contradicted by historical representations of beauty.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/03/hegel.jpg)

_Tanning: a loss for no gain._

Beyond the question of tanning, beauty and skin tone is an issue I'd prefer to [pass over in silence](https://www.jsanilac.com/milgram-questions/). However, to discourage misinterpretations, I'll address it briefly.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/aig-choker-cool.jpg)

Dark skin is extremely rare in all the datasets of imaginary women drawn on for this article. In the sets of [anime girls](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/12/popular.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) and [AI girls](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/10/aigirlcolor.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), dark skin never or virtually never appears among the most popular images. Top [uncensored](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/10/uncens_searchresults.png?ref=jsanilac.com) search [results](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/01/searchresultstop50.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) similarly omit dark skin. Just [1% of the sex dolls have black skin](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/doll-skin-tone.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), and those categorized as “brown” are mostly not dark at all, as you can see from the examples below.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/doll-brown.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/brown3.webp)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/brown2.webp)

_Three "brown" dolls._

_Left: warehouse-stocked dolls, USA. Right: warehouse-stocked dolls, EU. Center: red doll._

The images shown in _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ accurately reflect the most common types in the datasets of imaginary women just mentioned. That's why, for instance, no short-haired women are shown, and women with purple, pink, red, black, and blue skin are few in number. Manufacturing diversity where there isn't any simply to make this article more palatable would be dishonest reporting.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/aig-cook-3.jpg)

_Some countries generate more AI girls than others._

Nevertheless, it's important to note that none of these datasets come from countries where dark skin is the most common, and other confounds may exist as well. One could therefore argue that they don't constitute definitive evidence of men's skin-tone preferences in either direction. For these and several further reasons, I've chosen not to record any conclusions about the attractiveness of innate skin tones, however apparent those conclusions might seem to certain readers.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/ping-aig.jpg)

You can't change your innate skin tone, but you _can_ change how much you tan. Be smart.
