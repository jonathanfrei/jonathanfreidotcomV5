Here's a simplified checklist of the seven most effective ways to look more attractive. These may seem obvious, but _hardly any_ women today cover them all.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2021/09/011725-000_Voluptuositat.jpg)

> 1. Maintain a healthy weight. 2. Wear makeup. 3. Wear long hair. 4. Wear clothes that fit properly at the waist. 5. Do strength training focused on the glutes. 6. Get large breast implants if you're comfortable with the risks. 7. Get a nose job if you have a distinctly bad nose.
>
> Bonus: Learn to move attractively.

Start at the top and work down. 1, 4, 5, and 6 will help develop or enhance an hourglass shape. 2, 3, and 7 will take care of the rest.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/07/demi-boots.jpg)

_The loose waist of these jean shorts is an exception that proves the rule, because the bare midriff makes the model's waist visible._

Since seven is a more auspicious number than eight, I've listed motion as a bonus point. It actually has a multiplicative effect on all the others. Fear of seeming ridiculous during the learning curve prevents most women from taking what's actually an easy win. If you persist a short while and get it right, men won't be able to look away.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2021/11/fashion4.jpg)

_Implants and ruffled skirts with a fitted waist can create an hourglass figure for a woman who would otherwise seem too straight and thin._

Some women may feel uncomfortable to see surgery on the list. If you're morally opposed or have a low tolerance for risk, feel free to ignore it. Nevertheless, [breast augmentation](./025-3-cosmetics-and-cosmetic-surgery.md) is one of the most effective beauty interventions. Breast size has a larger effect on the attractiveness of slim women than any other single trait. That's why the recommended size can add a full point on the ten-point beauty scale. Omitting it from the list to make readers feel more comfortable would go against the high-minded principles that motivate this article.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/03/long-hair-truck.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/03/crosecrop.jpg)

_There's a lot you can do to increase your attractiveness before you pay for cosmetic surgery._

There's no point in getting your augmentation before you've [reached your best weight](./002-1-finding-the-best-weight-for-you.md), because men evaluate breast size in relation to waist size. The same holds for motions and gait. These only increase attractiveness for women who already have a good baseline. Otherwise they will fall flat. So I recommend you go through the list in order.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/06/compensating.jpg)

_The fashion industry wants you to think you can compensate with expensive designer handbags. You can't. They have zero effect on your attractiveness. Easily the most useless accessory._

Take a moment to consider just how little time and money is necessary to look more attractive. Only points six and seven on the list above require a significant commitment of resources. Most women aren't failing because they're not trying hard enough. They're failing because they're trying too hard to do the wrong things. Before you try harder, aim better.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/12/mudd-curl.jpg)

Women who are first coming to grips with the facts about feminine beauty sometimes jump to the conclusion that only a boob job really matters. That's not the case. There are six more points on this list of basics, and _many_ further techniques explained elsewhere in the article that will help to increase your attractiveness without adding silicone.

This top-seven list isn't exhaustive. However, as you go beyond it your returns will diminish. One of the most common beauty mistakes is to obsess over irrelevancies. Conventional beauty advice reinforces this mistake by encouraging you to worry about details that don't matter so you buy stuff that doesn't help.

I've kept it simple for good reason. Prioritize the basics and don't stress about the rest.

_Too much stressing and obsessing will make you look worse, not better._
