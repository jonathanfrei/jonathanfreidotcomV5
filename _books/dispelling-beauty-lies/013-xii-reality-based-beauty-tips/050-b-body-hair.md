Today at least some removal of body hair is widely considered a basic, unquestionable necessity for feminine beauty. Imaginary women support this view. They never have hairy legs, and very rarely have underarm hair. Shaving recreates a smooth, prepubescent appearance men seem to find attractive.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/aig-garter-crop.jpg)

_A rare pose, and frankly not very elegant. Images of imaginary women normally emphasize breasts, butts, thighs, and even feet, but not genitals. Female genitals aren't really comparable to male ones, and have less visual relevance than you might assume. You shouldn't worry about their appearance, let alone consider surgical alterations._

Readers have asked me to research pubic hair in detail. It's counterintuitive, but the genital area is not an important element of feminine beauty. Only a few percent of AI-girl images show it clearly. Going through several thousand to find those few percent and categorize the pubic hair of a statistically significant number isn't my idea of a good time.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/03/aig-purple.png)

_Camel toe appears reasonably often in images of AI girls, but unclothed genitals are rare except in representations of sex._

While I won't provide exact figures, my rough impression is that a hairless genital area is the most common, and untrimmed pubic hair is almost nonexistent. So your safest bet is to shave it entirely. Trimming it to be unobtrusive is a more convenient alternative. I especially recommend you avoid stubble, the “bacon strip” pattern, and any other shaving pattern that draws attention to itself.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/10/lorenbw.webp)

Both underarm hair and thick [pubic hair](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/08/jl-1.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) were considered unobjectionable in living memory, even for centerfold photo shoots. This suggests today's trend toward universal hairlessness is partly a matter of fashion.

Despite what I've written in the preceding paragraphs, the case for shaving body hair isn't as absolute as most people believe. There's been considerable historical and regional variation in shaving expectations, and we have clear evidence that [some men](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/arm-request.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) today prefer an unshaven look. Consider, for instance, the AI artists whose work is shown below.

_The hundreds of likes for these images show that underarm hair on women has erotic appeal for some men. Use caution though. Most others will strongly object._

You should be careful to avoid hairy legs, as these aren't featured in any representations of attractive women. And removing both underarm and pubic hair is currently the safe and advisable default. But leaving them unshaven isn't entirely out of the question, especially in places where that's customary.

_As discussed previously, men have a contradictory taste for both neoteny and sexual maturity. A major reason their preferences vary is that they value these contradictory elements to different degrees. Underarm hair, which is a sign of maturity, may be another example of such variation._

If you're tired of shaving, show your lover the images in this section and ask his opinion. But if you're single in a place where everyone shaves, the reward for going unshaven is unlikely to justify the risk.
