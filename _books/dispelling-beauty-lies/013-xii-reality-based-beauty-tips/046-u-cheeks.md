A beauty mistake often seen in fashion advertising is using cosmetic contouring and even buccal fat removal to create or enhance the appearance of hollow cheeks. Don't do it.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/buccal.jpg)

_Left is much better, and not just because of the age difference._

Like much of high fashion, this has a masculinizing effect. The large majority of men prefer full cheeks.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/aishwarya8.webp)

_All_ of the top celebrity faces men selected as their favorites have full cheeks. Those who suffer from an eating disorder and believe they need to lose “face fat” should pay particular attention to this very clear evidence. In reasonably slim women “face fat” is a positive, not a negative.

You can confirm this by reexamining the images in the [faces section](../008-vii-the-most-important-secret-about-faces/001-vii-the-most-important-secret-about-faces.md). One AI artist even dreamed up puffer-fish to satisfy his love for full cheeks.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/puffer2.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/flat23-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/puffer3.jpg)

And women with full cheeks aren't just more beautiful when young. They also tend to age better and look younger longer. In fact, a procedure to increase cheek fullness should make some older faces seem more youthful.

![](https://www.jsanilac.com/content/media/2025/08/salma-58_thumb.jpg)

[Video](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/media/2025/08/salma-58.mp4)

 _Age 58. Note the cheeks._

That women today are doing the reverse instead is yet another sad reminder of just how disconnected fashions can be from genuine beauty.
