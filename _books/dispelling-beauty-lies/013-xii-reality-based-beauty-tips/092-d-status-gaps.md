Bossy jobs aren't the only case where moving up means moving down. The heroines in male fantasies typically have lower social status than the hero, not higher. And women's fantasies are especially emphatic on this point. So when a woman's social status is too high, it reduces attraction on both sides. This applies to rank, wealth, fame, and all other dimensions of status.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/demi-queen.jpg)

_Are secretaries hotter than queens? Yes._

A taste for higher-status men has counterintuitive consequences. Increasing your own status shrinks the number of desirable men, because the higher it is, the fewer men are above you. As you approach the top you reduce even the best man's attractiveness, because there's no longer enough room for a status gap that can fully amplify his appeal.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/aig-ladder.jpg)

_Romance novels rarely feature heroines at the very top of the social ladder. Doing so precludes the possibility of a maximally attractive hero._

Thus, the ideal status for a woman is high enough to put desirable men in reach, but not so high that she matches or exceeds them. And to repeat, this follows from women's own preferences even more than from men's.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/tuba-crown-2-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/tuba-fall-2.jpg)

_Who is the true woman of no rank?_

Broadly speaking, if women are most attracted to men who are higher status and men are most attracted to women who are lower status, then relationship satisfaction can only be maximized in a society where women's status is structurally lower than men's. The chart below illustrates this point.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/status-pyramid2.jpg)

_Relationship satisfaction if a one-rank status gap in favor of men is optimal. Each level on the pyramid represents one rank, and the lines indicate relationships. Greater inequality will further improve satisfaction if we accept gaps in excess of one rank._

Fortunately, your own choices don't depend on whether society as a whole prioritizes relationships or equality. Peaks can be lonely places. If you put all your energy into climbing higher, don't be surprised when the outcome turns out to be less than you hoped for.
