When it comes to the fundamental emotions of care, desire, and enjoyment, authenticity is important. But precisely because men do want to see that you care about them, desire them, and enjoy their attention, inauthentic behavior that's motivated by care, desire, and enjoyment affects them positively, not negatively. In fact, inauthentic behavior can sometimes be _more_ seductive than authentic behavior. I've already mentioned coyness as an example, but there are others as well. Consult the tables below to see where authenticity matters.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/marilyn-hot.jpg)

Wanting to be feminine and seductive is attractive in itself, whereas [indifference](https://www.jsanilac.com/dominance/#ix-women-are-being-entrapped-in-aggressive-indifference-to-male-desire) to men is unattractive in itself. One reason inauthentic seduction techniques work is that their inauthenticity makes apparent the authentic desire to be seductive that motivates them.

> _What Doesn't Need To Seem Authentic_
>
> Coyness Fearfulness Vivaciousness Cuteness Airheadedness Aggression Pouting Roleplay Exaggerations of pleasure and femininity

>  _What Does Need To Seem Authentic_
>
> Desire Affection Enthusiasm Pleasure Femininity Quirkiness Fluidity Sex-positive attitude Sophisticated elegance Yin (minority taste)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/tuba-tiara.webp)

_Yin and sophisticated elegance need to seem authentic, while vivaciousness and cuteness don't. This is yet another reason why the former are such difficult seduction strategies._

Use caution. Being noticeably inauthentic where any of the items on the red list are concerned is very anti-seductive. And just because a technique is on the green list doesn't mean anything goes. It must meet two conditions to be excused from the need to seem authentic. First, it must seem motivated by the items on the red list. Second, it must have a fun and playful quality, with at least a hint of cuteness.

_Typically the more "serious" or "adult" your seduction strategy feels, the more authentic it should appear._

For example, exaggerating the pleasure you feel in a cute way that seems motivated by genuine enjoyment will have a positive effect, but exaggerating it in a serious, melodramatic way that seems disconnected from your real experience will have a negative effect. In fact, that's one of the worst mistakes you can make.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/brigitte-ruffled.png)

_Jerky or incongruous motions seem inauthentic in an off-putting way._

Attractive motions can be part of an inauthentic performance, but they still need to seem fluid and expressive. If they're stiff or you're just “going through the motions,” the inauthenticity will be grating.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/aig-train-7-4.jpg)

_Effortless yin. Don't try this at home._

> _Seductive Character Traits Summary_
>
> 1. A high libido is the single most attractive trait, especially when combined with bashfulness. 2. Cuteness, or even just being fun and playful, is almost always more seductive than sophisticated elegance. 3. Vivacious energy is almost always more seductive than mysterious darkness. 4. Desire and pleasure need to seem authentic, but cuteness and coyness are still seductive when they seem affected. 5. Frigidity, promiscuity, self-absorbed pride, and intelligence displayed at the wrong time are all turnoffs, and prudish frigidity is the worst. 6. There are many more ways to be seductive than I have time to describe. These are just the traits that work the most reliably on the most men.
