The yin-yang association of femininity with mysterious darkness is sanctified by tradition. But like many traditions (human sacrifice, circumcision, etc.), it’s wrong. Feminine attractiveness is more often vivacious light than mysterious darkness. To be clear, this is a matter of character and vibe, not clothing or skin color. Page through _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ and pay attention to the mood of the images. You’ll see it’s true.

The same holds for [traditional paintings](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/size/08/Biblis.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) of beautiful women. Glamour photographers use [mirrors](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/demi-pool-3-1.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) to throw as much light as possible on their models even when outdoors in bright sunlight.

Men usually find bright, youthful energy the most seductive. Mysterious darkness is a [minority](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/graveyard.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [taste](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/nice-boots.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) few women should be trying to cater to. Worse still, it's impossible for even the most mysterious woman to stay mysterious for the duration of a long-term relationship. This means that even when mysteriousness _does_ seduce men, its spell is doomed to wear off.

> _The Daylight Cluster_
>
> Luminosity, vivaciousness, cuteness, and girlishness form a related cluster of feminine traits that the large majority of men find attractive. To be seductive, most women should bring these traits out. Darkness, poise, and mysteriousness are a separate cluster of traits that's best understood as a minority taste. Yes, it can be seductive to the right men. But it's much harder to exploit, and you should only try if it especially suits you.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/marilyn-1954.webp)

_In the early stages of a relationship there can be advantages to leaving him uncertain about what you’re thinking or how you feel. But this is different from being “mysterious,” let alone shadowy. Trying too hard to seem mysterious often just comes off as pretentious and not particularly exciting._

Originality is normally unhelpful in beauty and seduction, because men like a narrow range of generic traits. Quirkiness is a rare exception to this rule, and it complements vivacious light especially well. Having one or two unique oddities in your behavior, attitudes, or habits increases your cute factor and makes you seem more spontaneous and lively. That said, leaning too hard into quirkiness could become annoying. So if you have natural quirks, let them show, but don't make an exaggerated parody of them.
