_Because both sexes have reasons not to fully explain their preferences, women underestimate how many men want them to offer fellatio regularly and men underestimate how many women enjoy giving it. This ought to be an easy win for everyone._

When it can take less time than a massage and provide more pleasure to both parties, the neglect seems perplexing. They wait for their lover to request it. And that turns what should be a big seductive advantage into a big missed opportunity.

_Becoming the kind of woman men fantasize about is easy, actually._

They want you to demonstrate your attraction by volunteering, and you can _only_ do that when they _don't_ ask. So you need to judge your lover's desires by his reactions, not his requests. _No matter how you put the question, the answer remains the same._

You might try to understand the effect fellatio has on men by comparing it to the effect cunnilingus would have on you. But the analogy doesn't work. Even if they create the same physical pleasure, fellatio has far more seductive power. That's because cunnilingus puts the "wrong" sex in the submissive role of giving it. This makes it psychologically very different, and even opposite. It's not an accident that cunnilingus doesn't feature prominently in romance novels, and is almost totally absent from vintage romances. Remember, men and women aren't symmetrical, but complementary, so what works on one never works quite the same on the other. In this case, fellatio will work much better than you expect.
