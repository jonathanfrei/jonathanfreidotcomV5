_Is she fantasizing about equality? Or, maybe not?_

> _Everyone Assumes They Understand Male Sexuality, But They Don't_
>
> Today's popular discourse on sex treats the nature of male pleasure and desire as obvious to everyone. I hope it's clear by now that this isn't the case. Everyone thinks they understand male sexuality, but they don't. They _mis_ understand it. Worse still, popular discourse fails to constructively address the consequences of aspects that really are obvious. Because these aspects seem morally problematic, they're impugned, or else swept under the rug where they don't have to be seen. This dismissive attitude hasn't helped anyone. Instead it's lead most people to overlook simple solutions that make happier relationships possible.

_Ignoring the fundamental differences between male and female desire sets you up for frustration._

One of the most important facts about seduction is that male and female pleasure, like masculine and feminine beauty, are inherently asymmetrical. Women's enjoyment of submission would be enough to make the conventional notion of equality inapplicable even if all else were the same. Yet other aspects of pleasure differ too.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/06/demi-syrup.jpg)

_Separate, unequal, and fun._

Women can experience an intense, extended sequence of orgasms, but men can only achieve the same amount of pleasure with shorter, less intense episodes of greater quantity and variety. To encourage that quantity and variety, Mother Nature subjects men to more consistently elevated desire, whereas women's desire waxes and wanes. Because of these and many other differences, identical and therefore “equal” behavior will always fail to satisfy _either_ sex.

_There are important differences between what works in movies and stories and what works in real life._

> _For Every Kiss You Give Me_
>
> In stories sex is continually reciprocal, with both lovers equally pleased at all times. Actors in romantic movies climax together, and climax the same number of times too. These are narrative conventions intended to represent the shared happiness of good relationships, but _not_ the way good relationships actually work. In real life, insisting on equal and simultaneous pleasure reduces that pleasure, leaving both lovers less fulfilled. It’s a terrible idea. Equality and simultaneity are things you simply shouldn’t care about. The only thing that matters for a relationship is mutual satisfaction in the long run.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/muddmirror-1.jpg)

Position 69 is reciprocal, simultaneous, and equal, and it's highly rated in popular lore. But it's actually ranked far below fellatio by both men _and_ women.

As soon as we stop pretending men and women are the same and move past the trap of superficial equality, it becomes easy to see how genuinely mutual satisfaction can be achieved. Most problems are caused by just three main sexual differences. _Quantity_ ,_quality_ , and _variety_.
