Here's a model with a mediocre bodyfat distribution not far from the average woman's. In this case gaining weight would do harm. But losing weight would eliminate the minimal curves she has and do harm as well. For instance, the difference between a flat chest and a small B cup is very important to men. If you can make that difference with good weight management, you should.

You might feel intimidated by the curvaceous figures praised in _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ because you don't have the genetics or surgical budget to imitate them effectively. However, as the example above shows, most women have some attractive curves when they're at their best weight. That said, a few unlucky women will still end up with an A cup. If you can't grow breasts without also growing a fatter middle than the right photo above, you should stay slim. Later on I'll discuss in detail how you can build curves through artificial means.
