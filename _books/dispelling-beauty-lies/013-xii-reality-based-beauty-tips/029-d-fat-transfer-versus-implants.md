There are two breast-augmentation methods available today: implants and fat transfer. Each has strengths and weaknesses. At first fat transfer might sound like the clear winner. However, because it can only move modest amounts of tissue at once, it requires several surgeries to achieve the same increase in volume implants provide in a single surgery. Furthermore, not all of the transferred fat stays where it's put in the long term.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/09/plaster-1.jpg)

_It would take many surgeries to achieve this size with fat transfer alone even if all goes well._

Implants are more consistent and provide a large increase in volume in a single surgery. They also retain their shape better over time, particularly at the upper pole. If you lose weight, your implant size won't change. However, implants tend to look less natural than fat transfer. This isn't always bad, because natural breasts often sag more than you'd like. But if your surgeon does an underwhelming job, the resulting shape may still be less than ideal.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/09/aig-jean-cook.jpg)

_Imaginary women typically have what I call a "too-good-to-be-true" look. It isn't quite natural, but it isn't unnatural either._

A third approach is to combine implants and fat transfer. In theory the fat transfer should make the implants more convincing, giving you the best of both worlds. In practice it's hard to get much fat to take in such circumstances.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/09/demi-bay2-1.jpg)

This look is likely a product of natural tissue, implants, _and_ multiple fat-transfer procedures.

Implants are the tried and true method of augmentation. Surgeons have perfected their implantation techniques over generations so they can give consistently attractive results. However, improvements to fat transfer make it more viable for breast augmentation than it was in the past. This is especially true if you're just looking for a small boost.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/09/b0F5oRGs.jpg)

_For a small boost in the realm of 100-200ccs, fat transfer makes sense._

At the time of writing, most surgeons consider implants preferable, and they're far more popular. So the discussion below focuses on choosing the right implants. The height and sizing examples should remain useful regardless of what breast-augmentation method you choose.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/09/aig-2905.jpg)

_Improvements to fat transfer will eventually give women full control over their breast size, albeit at considerable expense._
