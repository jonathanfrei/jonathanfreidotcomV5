_The four main lingerie colors. Black is the most popular, but not the best choice for every woman all the time._

Black and red create a different character impression than pink and white. This applies to clothing in general of course, but the difference is stronger when it comes to lingerie, so you should consider it when you choose colors. Black and red are adult and directly sexual. They're also good colors if you want to emphasize sophisticated elegance.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/monica24.jpg)

Whites, pale pinks, and mauves are cute, innocent, and neotenous. And that innocent impression persists even for elaborately lacy lingerie young girls would never wear.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/blonde-demi.jpg)

Some women who suffer from a Madonna-whore complex associate lacy lingerie with slutty behavior. This is [misguided in the first place](../010-ix-beauty-and-hotness/001-ix-beauty-and-hotness.md), but it's especially wrong for white lace, which makes a pure and innocent impression on men.

Lace is indispensable for white lingerie. Plain white bras with no lace tend to look boring or matronly, so you should avoid them. Lace bras that are more [open](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/lace-2334.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com), like the example below, look better than [thickly padded ones](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/bluebra.webp?ref=jsanilac.com), at least when your clothes are off. However, the difference isn't that big, and convenience matters too.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/02/mudd-white-lace.jpg)

_Choose white lingerie that includes a meaningful amount of lace, though not necessarily this much._

Panties with a juvenile printed design have a neotenous effect with high sex appeal. This is a powerful trick for women who can pull it off. It works best in white and pink, but baby blue, turquoise, and lavender are also possible.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/03/juvenile-print-1.jpg)

_Ultra-cute tricks like juvenile-print lingerie are a big turn on for many men, but some find them annoying instead. They're worth trying despite the risk because the upside is very good._

These prints are an acceptable alternative to lace, and much more attractive than plain basics. Recall also that as you have smaller curves, it becomes more important to emphasize neoteny. Printed designs aren't forbidden to curvaceous women. But they're especially impactful for girls who are average or rather flat, and not too tall.
