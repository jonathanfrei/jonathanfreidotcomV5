You might try a few of the beauty and seduction techniques listed in _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ , not notice a reaction when you're out and about, and conclude they're not working. That conclusion would be much too hasty. Signs of attention and inattention can be misleading. I'll explain why in the next few paragraphs.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/night-out.jpg)

_Lower-cut tops are indeed more attractive, but the effect size is smaller than reactions suggest, especially in the long term._

Because decolletage and visible nipple imprints always pull in glances, it's easy to overestimate how much they really increase your ability to win over the men you want. Men feel an instinctive psychological compulsion to look, in the same way your leg kicks when a doctor taps your knee. In other words, their glances are first and foremost a reflex. Some interested men may resist that reflex out of politeness, and some uninterested men, including platonic friends, may not resist it. Either way, it doesn't mean as much as it seems to.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/ruffled-skirt-2.jpg)

_Humans don't come with a rear-view mirror._

Elsewhere underestimates are more common. You rarely notice glances at your rear, because you don't have eyes in the back of your head. This means rear motions and a swaying gait are both more effective than they seem. And men aren't going to stare at your hoop earrings or your choker with their mouths open even when these succeed at increasing your attractiveness. That's just not how it works. Now, obviously the fact that you don't notice an effect doesn't prove there is one. But it doesn't prove there isn't one either.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/stewardess-aig-2.jpg)

The effect her unbuttoned top has on men will be noticeable in stray glances, whereas the effect her neckerchief has won't be noticeable. The neckerchief _is_ having an effect nonetheless. Incidentally, although it suits this image well, a neckerchief is usually not as good as other choker types.

In short, you shouldn't give too much weight to the presence or absence of glances and stares. Besides which, not all stares are good. The absence of cold approaches doesn't prove much either, because men in some parts of the world now assume these will be poorly received, and so never venture them even on women they find especially attractive.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/ruffled-skirt-32.jpg)

_Modern life: waiting for approaches that never come._

Most beautification and seduction techniques have a small effect size that's almost unnoticeable on its own. But the key phrase is “on its own.” It's the cumulative impact of many small things that meaningfully increases your attractiveness, not one or two changes in isolation. Unfortunately that makes it very hard to figure out what's working and what isn't.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/beach-thigh-3.jpg)

_Reactions to your gait aren't going to be clear enough to serve as a useful guide for refining a decent gait into a great one. To improve your gait you should either try to imitate ideal examples more closely, or ask for corrections from a man you trust who has a good eye for beauty. The same goes for other attractive motions._

The [seduction quiz](https://www.jsanilac.com/101/#seduction-quiz) gives estimates for how big an effect you can expect from the main beautification and seduction techniques. The numbers are very approximate and you shouldn't take them too seriously, but they're still helpful. For the techniques I've valued at one point, you usually won't be able to observe any change in men's reaction. As you can see, most of the clothing and style items women wish would have a big impact are in this category.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/thigh-high.jpg)

_Thigh-highs have a larger effect than other articles of clothing, so there's a chance you'll notice a reaction when you wear them._

However, if you add _many_ individual points to your seduction score, you should be able to observe a collective difference in how men react to you. Enough unnoticeable changes amount to a noticeable one.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/irresistible-1.jpg)

_Complete irresistibility wasn't built in a day._

To determine whether your efforts are yielding results, compare the reactions you used to get to the reactions you get after you improve your seduction score by at least forty points. You might need more than that to make a difference if you're starting from a low baseline. Don't expect men to fall at your feet over a few minor improvements. That's not going to happen.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/demi-fan.jpg)

_Adding or removing the earring from this photo will have an almost undetectable effect on men's reaction to it, but that doesn't mean it does nothing at all. Great artworks rise above mediocre ones because of innumerable small and almost imperceptible improvements like this. Discerning which details help and which hurt is part of the challenge._
