Excessive lip fillers are sometimes intentionally used by glamour models because they pop well on phone screens. The same can be said for false eyelashes, which often look good in photos when they're implausibly large. In real life these excesses rarely work.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/rk4rcop.jpg)

_These lips are fine, but more would be too much. The eyelashes are good in a photo, but would be overkill in real life._

That said, both lip fillers and false eyelashes are now unfairly maligned because a few women have overdone them so egregiously. In moderation, they look good on the right person. You can see an example of lips that are very plump but not excessive [here](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/10/cigar.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com).
