I'm not sure why I have to be the one to explain these basics about the birds and the bees. But for whatever reason, no one has written them down properly, and the modern tendency to conflate symmetrical behavior with equal satisfaction has confused people's thinking about matters everyone should understand.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/08/field.jpg)

Simply falling back on instinct isn't enough to fix this, because male and female instincts are imperfectly aligned for mutual satisfaction. So inexperienced couples end up fumbling in the dark unnecessarily, sometimes for years on end, which is a great waste. Making our relationships the best they can be requires knowledge and a little effort, not just instinct or luck.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/aishwarya9crop.png)

_If you doubt the accuracy of these recommendations, test them before you assert that they're wrong._

No explanation will quiet the shrieks of the most insistent critics, who will endlessly invent moralistic reasons everyone should be as miserable as they are. But this advice isn't written for them. It's written for women who do care about mutual satisfaction and enjoy creating it. And for women who think becoming a goddess of love sounds like, well, a rather tempting idea. If you'd prefer to engage in moral grandstanding or go shopping, you're reading the wrong article.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/seductioneueu.jpg)

_Peak human fulfillment, surely._

Seduction is a complex topic that merits a full article of its own, which will, alas, never be written. For lack of time I've described it in broad strokes using many rough generalizations, and it's up to you to work out the specifics. Not all men have the same tastes. For instance, a small minority enjoy being dominated by women, and will respond well to aggressive assertiveness that other men only appreciate as occasional variety. Experiment, then judge your success by his reactions. As with attractive motions, the unavoidable short-term risk of embarrassment or awkwardness is more than justified by the long-term benefits you'll enjoy after you get it right.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/06/demi-banana-2.jpg)

_I'm convinced some influencers and writers try to normalize bizarre behaviors and denigrate "vanilla" (read: normal) sex just to mess with people. Beware of advice that's either too timid or too determinedly transgressive. Neither hits the mark._

> No seduction or beauty technique can make a relationship work if it's not meant to be. Love is a game people usually lose. _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ is only intended to tilt the odds in your favor. Even the best advice can't guarantee success.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/love-trip-crop.jpg)

_If a guy really just doesn't like you that much, doubling down on seduction techniques is not going to save your relationship. Instead you'll end up feeling used and degraded for trying too hard. Know when it's time to move on. Also, consider that he might be going through work or health problems that are getting in the way. Seduction will help a little, but it can't fix these._
