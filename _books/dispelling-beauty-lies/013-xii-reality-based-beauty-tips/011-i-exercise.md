Exercise is less important to maintain a healthy weight than diet, but still worth some effort.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/07/fit-girl-crop.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/pullupcrop.jpg)

_Lower-body exercises have a better aesthetic impact than upper-body exercises._

As far as beauty is concerned, it's best to skip upper-body exercise entirely. Only a small fraction of men find defined abs attractive, and [broad shoulders](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/04/tpoll4.png?ref=jsanilac.com) and [muscular](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/11/musclegirl.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) [arms](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/character-creation.png?ref=jsanilac.com) are more negative than positive. A little shoulder and arm development is okay. But any more can have a markedly bad, masculinizing effect on your whole figure. This is even more true if you have naturally broad shoulders. Idealized imaginary women usually have thin and undeveloped arms and shoulders [regardless of how thick](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/02/aix2-1.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) their bodies are. A muscular back is less problematic, though still not advisable.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/aig-computer-2.jpg)

_Imaginary women never have gymnast shoulders._

Some women overdo their upper body accidentally by participating in the wrong sports. If you want to be involved in athletics, the best sports for beauty are short-distance track-and-field events, such as sprinting. Long-distance running, sometimes recommended for weight loss, is not fantastic for overall beauty, and gymnastics develops the upper body too much.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/marcel-pouret.jpg)

_An hourglass figure wins first place for beauty, but isn't competitive in athletics._

Be aware that the most attractive female body type can't win first place at sports, where wide hips and large breasts are a competitive disadvantage. Medal-winning female athletes are usually too muscular as well. It's best to think of beauty as its own sport. When you play other sports, play for fun, and don't worry about winning.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/09/from-isabel.png)

_Persian love goddess, circa 400 AD, featuring a projecting rear that's more prominent than the rest of her legs._

While upper-body exercises aren't recommended, lower-body exercises can be quite beneficial. However, they're best targeted to specific parts of the lower body. Overdeveloping your glutes relative to the rest of your legs is advantageous. In fact, a glutes-only strength-training regimen may actually work very well. Personal trainers and athletes will be appalled. But if you're training for beauty, real, “functional” strength isn't the goal. You want to look hotter, not win at sportsball.

Some will dismiss this model as exaggerated, but she's very popular with men and exemplifies a true fact. The most attractive lower body [isn't proportionate](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/02/largerearshape.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com).

For a minimal exercise routine, try doing three sets of glutes-targeted resistance training three times a week, and no other weightlifting. For health and fitness you can supplement this with some form of light exercise. If you're more ambitious, you can increase your glutes training volume or mix in sprints.
