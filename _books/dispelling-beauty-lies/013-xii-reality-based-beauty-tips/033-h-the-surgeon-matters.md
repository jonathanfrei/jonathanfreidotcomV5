Breast augmentations have gotten better and more consistent over time, but even today there's a chance of botched or mediocre work. Not all surgeons are good at what they do, so it's important to research thoroughly before you commit. Needless to say, there's a massive difference between a good boob job and a botched one.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/10/aig-263.jpg)

_I never provide recommendations for particular surgeons, whether in this article or in private consultation. Finding a good one is entirely your responsibility._

First study all the linked images in this section so you know what ideal breasts should look like. The videos in the later section on motion are also good reference points. Then ask for clear photos of each surgeon's previous patients and compare their results to the examples I've provided. Bad augmentations tend to look unnaturally spherical, with the outlines of the implant too apparent. Avoid surgeons whose results have these flaws. Talk to patients about their experience if you can.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2023/10/mccalla-vintage.jpg)

_Before the development of breast augmentation this was a one-in-a-million figure. Today you can buy it._

When it comes to expensive home repairs, standard advice is to get opinions and quotes from three experts before making a decision. The same practice makes good sense when it comes to your body. Don't choose a surgeon at random and hope for luck, or you might be sorely disappointed.
