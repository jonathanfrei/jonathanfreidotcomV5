Men’s ignorance about makeup is similar to their ignorance about surgery. Don’t be misled. Those who claim to prefer women without makeup actually prefer “natural-looking” makeup, but don’t realize it. The reality is that all women look better in makeup. Men's beauty advice is sometimes hard to distinguish from sabotage.

Fortunately you can get most of the benefits with a simple and efficient routine that doesn’t require hours every day. Makeup techniques are best learned from instructional videos, so I won't go into detail here. With experience and effort, most women do their makeup reasonably well. Below I'll just highlight a few common errors.

Red lipstick makes a huge difference for some faces._

Heavy Instagram-style makeup is meant to be effective when viewed on a small phone screen. To avoid looking clownish, make sure to tone it down for real life. The example below is wearing a little too much eyeshadow.

> _Makeup Is A Solved Problem_
>
> The basics of effective makeup have been the same for many generations now. Most new makeup trends break something that was working just to be different. If you use well-established, standard methods, you'll get a good result. Yes, it's fine to match the times with some minor updates. But gimmicky novelties are much more likely to make your face look worse than better. It's the wrong place to innovate.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/carp.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/07/sabrina2-crop.jpg)

_This celebrity has a pretty face but made herself look clownish with awful makeup that was apparently intended as a fashion statement. Fashion statements that detract from your beauty to this degree are not worth making. Also, avoid brown lipstick. Lipstick should be pink or red, never brown._

It's surprisingly common for women to do their eyebrows wrong. It's not necessary for everyone to cultivate an arch or a sharp point, and trying to force your eyebrows into a shape that doesn't suit your face leads to poor results. The important thing is to make sure they look dense and well defined. Don't make them into a thin line on purpose. The eyebrows shown at the left below are almost never seen in AI-girl faces.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/03/eyebrows.jpg)

_Men prefer the center eyebrows to the left eyebrows by a significant margin. Right: AI girls have dense, reasonably thick, and well-defined eyebrows of various shapes._

Most other people, especially men but other women too, are unable to fully distinguish your makeup from your face. Because of this I recommend you wear at least basic makeup at _all_ times, without any exceptions. This convinces everyone that your face is “naturally” better than it really is. And it’s especially important for older women. Some women intentionally go out without makeup, as if they’re proving some important point. Well, they’re not. They’re just convincing others that they’re plainer for no reason or benefit.
