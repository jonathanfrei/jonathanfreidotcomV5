You shouldn't rely on cup letters when selecting your size. Cup letters can be misleading, and the bra sizes cited by surgeons are often smaller than those buxom women report to fit comfortably.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2024/12/beach54oe.jpg)

Breast augmentation takes less time than a male model spends bodybuilding on a _single day_. In the game of life, it's the closest thing to a cheat code there is.

It's also important not to fixate on numbers in isolation. 800cc implants usually create an unnatural shape on a woman with an average frame, because they're more than her chest can comfortably fit. But on a tall woman with a wide chest, they may not even look big at all. For instance, the model on the left below actually has larger implants than the model on the right.

Post-op photos from a plastic surgeon's website. The tall model on the left has 750cc implants. Because of her body proportions they only look medium sized, not big. The second model is short and already had somewhat large breasts before her 600cc augmentation. By consequence her result looks much bigger than the [typical outcome](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2022/07/600-650cc_examples.jpg?ref=jsanilac.com) for that implant size. Be aware that camera angles and distance can change the apparent size in photos as well.

_This table is for the sake of illustration only. The implant size your body can fit has to be determined by your surgeon after an examination._

Because height isn't the only relevant variable, the chart won't apply to every case. Consider [_this informative before-and-after comparison_](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/implant-height.png?ref=jsanilac.com). The taller woman still looks much flatter than the shorter woman despite nearly double the implant volume. Always choose implants by how they'll look on your individual body. Not by how big or small the numbers and letters sound.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/04/aig-hall.jpg)

_Limitations in implant technology make it harder for tall women to achieve visually large sizes. This is especially true for tall women who have a narrow chest. In contrast, short women with a wide chest have access to the largest relative sizes._

> _Beware Advice From Frenemies_
>
> As soon as women realize how much size matters they become competitive about it. A sadly common story is for a friend to talk you down a size and then get herself a larger augmentation later. Your friends might wish you well most of the time, but they still don't want you to be hotter than them. So be careful whom you trust for advice about your augmentation.

Rice sizers help to visualize how larger breasts will look on you. You can find instructions for making them online. Just keep adding rice until you resemble different images in this article, and see how you feel. Unfortunately, their name is a little misleading. Rice sizers aren't a very accurate guide to sizing. Because they sit differently than implants, they can misrepresent sizes by 150ccs. Usually they look bigger than implants, but this isn't a hard rule. So make sure to discuss your desired look with your surgeon and show him photo examples of what you want before you make your final decision.
