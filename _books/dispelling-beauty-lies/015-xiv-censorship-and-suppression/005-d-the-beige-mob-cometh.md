I'd have liked to preserve my innocence and end this section here. And yet while the history of suppression recounted above is accurate, it's not complete. The final stroke arrived when a series of social-media posts regarding my views on beige lingerie slipped out of the pit in which I was buried and went unexpectedly viral, accruing a total of sixteen million views in a short span of time. This attention was, however, almost wholly negative.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/beige-fiasco-crop-1.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/skin.jpg)

_It doesn't matter how many millions of people shout the opposite on social media. Wearing underwear that matches your skin tone is, was, and will continue to be both unattractive and unnecessary. It's a burn-victim effect. And willful obtuseness won't change it._

My [views on beige lingerie](../013-xii-reality-based-beauty-tips/021-g-lingerie-color.md) stand among my most useful, most readily verified, most easily implemented, and also least offensive beauty advice. And yet the public formed a crazed mob to reject them unexamined. Rather than confront any of my simple observations, they issued a never-ending stream of baseless ad hominem insults. Woman after woman attacked me in a frenzy to avoid acknowledging the obvious fact that she'd spent years wearing ugly, and often expensive, underwear for no reason at all.

The thread in question summarized my views on beige lingerie, stated earlier in _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ , and concluded with the observation that avoiding it entirely was the best way to preserve one's seductive aura. All of social media aligned in lockstep to declare that the empress has no ugly bras. Public reaction was more than 95% negative. I encourage you to sample the replies.

This fiasco revealed two things. First, artificial suppression isn't the only reason _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ failed to draw the attention it deserves. Second, a large fraction of women today feel an extreme lack of interest in doing anything whatsoever to look better and make their mates happier, as well as an extreme degree of ill will toward anyone suggesting such improvements could even be made. "[Aggressive indifference](https://www.jsanilac.com/dominance/#ix-women-are-being-entrapped-in-aggressive-indifference-to-male-desire)" is the correct analysis, and yet my account of it understates their attitude.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/fashionably-boring.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/demi-white.jpg)

_Today the fraction of women who genuinely want to be more attractive is vanishingly small._

Women are creatures of pride, vanity, and love, and in a happy society the third counterbalances the first two. We do not, however, live in such a society. The beige mob is loveless, and uninterested in the truth. If it doesn't feed their pride and vanity, they do not care. Further still, they take pride in not caring, as if lovelessness were some kind of accomplishment. The messenger has to be shot, and then trampled.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/cinder.jpg)

_Once upon a time there was a beautiful girl with an evil stepmother. The stepmother would only buy her beige bras. When the girl met a passing prince, she begged for better. But her stepsisters exclaimed, “You can't have a pretty pink bra! We deserve the prince all for ourselves.”_

All this demonstrates exactly why [the beige-lingerie test](https://www.jsanilac.com/dominance/#ix-women-are-being-entrapped-in-aggressive-indifference-to-male-desire) is so valuable for men. A relationship with a woman who doesn't even care enough about her lover to change her bra color for him isn't a relationship at all, except perhaps one between a doormat and a foot. And a surprising fraction of women cannot pass this extremely easy test.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/ugly-underwear-2.png)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/mudd-maroon.jpg)

_Discovering that your wife never really cared about you when you're halfway through your marriage really hurts. That's why I advise married men to think twice before giving the beige-lingerie test._

Sadly though, men are part of the problem. Many have joined the beige mob. Supine men and loveless, overbearing women do not a happy society make.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/demi-feather.webp)

I'm thus forced to conclude my work on _Dispelling Beauty Lies_ with the sad report that the number of women who care about genuine beauty and seduction is too small to have justified its composition. If you, dear reader, are one of those few, your success as a seductress is almost guaranteed, because you have no competition. You could shout every seduction trick from the rooftops and your sisters would only plug their ears. I may have won nothing, but any woman who made it this far is about to enjoy a field day.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/75/49/75490b33-7444-4a11-886d-0e9d3cca15d9/content/images/2025/12/jayne-23.jpg)

_Are you ready for liftoff?_
