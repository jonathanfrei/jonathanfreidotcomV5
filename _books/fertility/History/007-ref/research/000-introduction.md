## Source cards

### 1 — OECD elasticity (already in chapter 5)

- **Use for:** Policy is too small.
- **Kind:** Secondary (OECD working paper).
- **Cite:** Jonas Fluchtmann, Violetta van Veen, and Willem Adema, “Fertility, Employment and Family Policy,” OECD Social, Employment and Migration Working Papers no. 299, 2023.
- **Link:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/fertility-employment-and-family-policy_326844f0-en.html
- **Claim:** An additional $1,000 per child (PPP) in leave, childcare, or family allowances is associated with roughly a 1 to 1.6 percent increase in the TFR.
- **Caveat:** Association, not a dial. Enough to recover births at the margin; nowhere near 1.4 → 2.1.
- **Already in the book?** Yes — chapter 5, “What governments have tried to raise fertility.” Point back.

### 2 — Hungary rise and fade (already in chapter 5)

- **Use for:** A country that went further still stopped short of replacement.
- **Kind:** Primary (KSH) + OECD.
- **Cite:** Hungarian Central Statistical Office, “Live births, total fertility rate”; OECD *Economic Surveys: Hungary 2026*.
- **Claim:** TFR about 1.23 in 2011 → 1.59 in 2021, then reversed. Family spending above 3 percent of GDP by OECD measure.
- **Already in the book?** Yes. One sentence.

### 5 — Livy 1.9.1 — the one-generation city

- **Use for:** Cold-open first sentence.
- **Kind:** Primary.
- **Cite:** Livy, *Ab Urbe Condita* 1.9. English: Rev. Canon Roberts, *History of Rome* (New York: E. P. Dutton, 1912).
- **Link:** https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.02.0026:book=1:chapter=9
- **Quote:** “The Roman State had now become so strong that it was a match for any of its neighbours in war, but its greatness threatened to last for only one generation, since through the absence of women there was no hope of offspring, and there was no right of intermarriage with their neighbours.”
- **Claim:** Livy states the problem as *penuria mulierum* and missing *conubium*, not as a missing baby bonus.
- **Caveat:** Foundation myth. Livy’s preface says he will neither affirm nor refute traditions before the city was built. Do not write as if this happened in 753 BC.

### 6 — Livy 1.9.10 — the festival

- **Use for:** The act.
- **Kind:** Primary. Same citation as card 5.
- **Quote:** “When the hour for the games had come, and their eyes and minds were alike riveted on the spectacle before them, the preconcerted signal was given and the Roman youth dashed in all directions to carry off the maidens who were present.”
- **Claim:** The seizure is planned (*ex composito*), timed to hospitality and a religious festival (Consualia).

### 7 — Livy 1.9.14–15 — Romulus’s speech

- **Use for:** How the legend converts violence into a fertility policy.
- **Kind:** Primary. Same citation.
- **Quote:** Romulus “pointed out to them that it was all owing to the pride of their parents in denying right of intermarriage to their neighbours. They would live in honourable wedlock, and share all their property and civil rights, and — dearest of all to human nature — would be the mothers of freemen.”
- **Claim:** The founder’s defense is demographic and civic: lawful marriage, property, citizenship, freeborn children. Cost is named and then overwritten.
- **Vocabulary:** English “Rape of the Sabine Women” translates *raptus* / *rapio* (seizure). Livy presents motive as population, not lust. Do not flatten either fact.
