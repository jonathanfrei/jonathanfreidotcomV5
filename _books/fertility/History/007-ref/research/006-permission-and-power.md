## Source cards

### 1 — *Evangelium Vitae* (already in chapter 1)

- **Use for:** Unborn life has a claim that does not depend on whether the pregnancy was planned; no demographic target authorizes killing.
- **Kind:** Primary (encyclical).
- **Cite:** John Paul II, *Evangelium Vitae*, nos. 57–63, 1995.
- **Link:** https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_25031995_evangelium-vitae.html
- **Already in the book?** Chapter 1. Name the tradition here only because the argument is now about permission.

### 2 — Compendium, family as first society (already in chapter 6)

- **Use for:** Subsidiarity: the state assists families; it does not absorb them. Solidarity: families are not abandoned with every cost.
- **Kind:** Primary (Holy See).
- **Cite:** Pontifical Council for Justice and Peace, *Compendium of the Social Doctrine of the Church*, ch. 5, 2004.
- **Link:** https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/pontifical_councils/justpeace/documents/rc_pc_justpeace_doc_20060526_compendio-dott-soc_en.html
- **Already in the book?** Chapter 6, social foundations.
