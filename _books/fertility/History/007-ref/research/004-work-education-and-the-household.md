### 4 — IZA: female education and fertility

- **Use for:** Mechanisms, including incarceration of teenage years in school.
- **Kind:** Secondary review.
- **Cite:** Jungho Kim, “Female education and its impact on fertility,” IZA *World of Labor*, art. 228 (updated).
- **Link:** https://wol.iza.org/articles/female-education-and-its-impact-on-fertility/long
- **PDF:** https://wol.iza.org/uploads/articles/651/pdfs/female-education-and-its-impact-on-fertility.pdf?v=1
- **Mechanisms to keep separate:**
  1. Opportunity cost / forgone earnings.
  2. Child “quality” investment.
  3. Health and child survival (more important in poor countries).
  4. Contraceptive knowledge and bargaining.
  5. **Incarceration effect:** Norway’s compulsory-school reform: +0.1 years schooling, **teenage pregnancy −8 percentage points**; completed fertility unchanged (women already postponed into the 20s–30s).
  6. Israel 1963 lifting of Arab travel restrictions: +1 year girls’ schooling, **−0.6 children**, **no change in female LFP** (~15%). Education can cut fertility without putting women in offices.
  7. Nigeria UPE: +1 year → **−0.26 children before age 25**.
  8. U.S. college access: female college graduates **~20% fewer children** than high-school graduates in one design; another (school-entry date) finds little effect — population and period matter.
- **High-income twist:** Negative education–fertility slope flattens or U-shapes where paid childcare is cheap relative to highly educated women’s wages.
- **Caveat:** Review of heterogeneous quasi-experiments. Do not write “education always does X.”

### 5 — Goldin, *Career and Family*

- **Use for:** Greedy jobs and the on-call parent.
- **Kind:** Secondary (book).
- **Cite:** Claudia Goldin, *Career and Family: Women’s Century-Long Journey toward Equity* (Princeton, 2021). Related: Goldin, “Career and Family: College Women Look to the Past,” NBER w5188, 1995.
- **Claim:** Time-intensive, on-call jobs pay a large premium. If someone must be on call at home, the couple often cannot both take the greedy job. The fertility cost is not “women learned to read.” It is a labor-market design that punishes interruption in the same years as childbearing.
- **Career cost of children (related literature):** Adda, Dustmann, Stevens, *JPE* 2017, ~35% of a woman’s lifetime earnings in their calibration — mostly interruptions and part-time, rest wage/skill.

### 7 — Chesterton, family wage

- **Use for:** The neighboring experiment: a wage that covers a family, versus shrinking the family to fit the wage.
- **Kind:** Primary. Same essay as the status section.
- **Link:** https://www.gkc.org.uk/gkc/books/Social_Reform_B.C.html
- **Claim:** “If you can make the wage larger, there is no need to make the family smaller. If you can make the family small, there is no need to make the wage larger.” Catholic Democrats’ “Family Wage” as the contrary of birth control.
- **Quote (second, if needed):** He cannot see how “the right to earn outside the home” under a richer stranger is freer than companionship with a freely accepted husband.
