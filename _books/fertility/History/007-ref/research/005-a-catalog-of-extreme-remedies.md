# Research: A catalog of extreme remedies

## What this section has to establish

Contemporary and speculative tools, evaluated, not pre-filtered. History is already told. Singapore is chapter 5 in 2026 clothes. Pension credits exist. Childless taxes have precedents. Higher-parity targeting has a logic (Sarris). Direct control and industrial reproduction stay on the board.

## Suggested order of evidence

Singapore 2026 package (primary, short) → German *Kindererziehungszeiten* as the cousin of a childless tax → IFS Birthday Accounts / care credits (they already went “out of the box”) → bachelor-tax precedents → abortion magnitude (already in ch. 5) → IVG/ectogenesis as speculative → quotas as China in reverse.

## Source cards

### 1 — Singapore NDR 2026 (primary)

- **Use for:** The latest respectable stack, still “make it easier.”
- **Kind:** Primary government.
- **Cite:** Strategy Group, PMO, “Marriage & Parenthood Measures at National Day Rally 2026,” 23 Aug 2026.
- **Link:** https://www.population.gov.sg/marriage-parenthood-measures-at-national-day-rally-2026/
- **Also:** https://www.madeforfamilies.gov.sg/ndr-2026-supporting-families
- **Numbers (confirmed against gov.sg, not the X recap):**
  - **SG Child Support Package** replaces Baby Bonus and Large Families Scheme: up to **S$62,000** per citizen child, **same for every birth order**.
  - Pieces: **S$10,000** Baby Gift in year one (two tranches); **S$2,000/year** ages 1–16 (**S$32,000**); **S$5,000** CDA First Step; up to **S$5,000** CDA co-match; **S$10,000** PSEA top-up at 17.
  - With existing MediSave Grant (S$5,000) and Edusave (~S$2,500): about **S$70,000** from birth to 17.
  - Childcare leave: **8 / 10 / 12 days** per working parent for 1 / 2 / 3+ citizen children aged 12 and under. Government reimburses statutory child-related leave up to the cap.
  - **Full-day childcare S$150/month and infant care S$300/month** at government-supported centres, **target by 2030**, rolled out from 2028. Confirmed in Annex C.
  - Housing: from Feb 2027 sales, first-timer families get **one extra BTO/SBF ballot chance per citizen child** 18 and under.
  - Full preschool subsidies to be extended **regardless of the applicant’s working status** (details 2027) — relevant to the stay-home vs dual-earner fight.
- **Claim:** This is not a rumor. It is also not a demonstrated path back to replacement. TFR 0.87 in 2025 was the X Freeze context; verify DOS if using that number in prose.
- **Do not:** Rebuild France/Hungary/Korea.

### 2 — German childrearing pension credits (primary)

- **Use for:** “Retool social security” already exists in a mild form.
- **Kind:** Primary (Deutsche Rentenversicherung).
- **Cite:** Deutsche Rentenversicherung, “Kindererziehung: Ihr Plus für die Rente.”
- **Link:** https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Rente/Familie-und-Kinder/Kindererziehung
- **Claim:** For children born **1992 or later**, up to **3 years** (36 months) *Kindererziehungszeiten* per child, credited at average earnings (Entgeltpunkte). For children before 1992, 2.5 years after Mütterrente II. Additional *Berücksichtigungszeiten* to the child’s 10th birthday. Assigned to the parent who predominantly raised the child.
- **Magnitude:** IFS 2026 back-of-envelope on a U.S. care credit: maybe **1–2%** fertility — fairness more than a boom. Use that honesty.

### 3 — IFS 2026 “out of the box” menu

- **Use for:** A mainstream pronatalist shop already proposing five- and six-figure baby bonuses and celebrity interventions.
- **Kind:** Same report as status section.
- **Link:** https://ifstudies.org/report-brief/the-demographic-dead-end-2026-state-of-fertility-report
- **Items:** American Birthday Accounts (seed ~$15–20k, unlock at births; they estimate 20–40% cohort fertility response from foreign cash studies — **aggressive; treat as their claim**); Child Caregiver Credits for Social Security; end marriage penalties; family housing via bedroom-linked grants; family-impact budgeting; celebrity culture; “civil godparents.”
- **Quote for the thought experiment:** They “would not endorse” paying Taylor Swift a billion dollars to have children, “but it is empirically possible” that it would produce more children than the same money in tax credits.
- **Caveat:** Do not launder their 20–40% as a finding. It is an extrapolation.

### 5 — Bachelor and childless taxes (precedent, use lightly)

- **Rome:** *aes uxorium*, a tax on unmarried men attributed to the censors. Sources are antiquarian and thin. One sentence or skip.
- **Fascist Italy:** bachelor tax 1926–27 under Mussolini. Standard in the natalist-policy literature.
- **USSR:** tax on bachelors, single women, and small families, 1941 (war finance + natalism).
- **Claim:** Taxing the unmarried or childless is not a 2026 invention. Humiliation is often the point; revenue is the cover.
- **Molson Hart** (thought experiment): tax the childless on every paycheck; parent-priority queues; comic sumptuary rules.

### 6 — Higher parity (Sarris)

- **Use for:** Target the 3rd/4th, not the conversion of DINKs.
- **Kind:** Public argument, not a paper.
- **Link:** https://x.com/simonsarris/status/1855447180439351773
- **Claim to test, not to cite as data:** Families with 2+ have less inertia; around 4, older children start to carry the household. Singapore 2026 **removed** birth-order tiering — interesting tension with Sarris.

### 7 — Abortion as a birth mechanism (already in chapter 5)

- **Cite:** Levine et al. on pre-*Roe* legalization (~5% birth decline); Ananat, Gruber, Levine on lifetime births; Bell et al., *JAMA* 2024, on *Dobbs* bans.
- **Already in the book?** Yes. One hard paragraph: law changes live births; the moral claim is not a TFR trick; the state still may not conscript conception.

### 8 — Title X / contraception (already in chapter 5)

- **Cite:** Martha Bailey, *AEJ: Applied* 2012, on federally funded family planning reducing childbearing among poor women.
- **Use for:** Ending public subsidy of fertility reduction ≠ a contraceptive police state.

### 9 — China one-child (already in chapter 5)

- **Use for:** Quota in reverse. CECC 2016; NBS communiqué on 2025 births (9.54 million 2024 → 7.92 million 2025).
- **Already in the book?** Yes.

### 10 — Industrial reproduction (speculative; keep short)

- **IVG / in-vitro gametogenesis:** mouse demonstrations; human research early. Review literature exists (e.g. *Nature*, *Cell*); do not claim a working human fertility program.
- **Ectogenesis / artificial womb:** lamb models (Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia “biobag,” 2017); human extra-uterine systems remain experimental and aimed at extreme prematurity, not elective gestation.
- **Claim for the catalog:** The technological path is real enough to be thinkable. Healing infertility ≠ manufacturing people to specification. Sorting belongs in permission.
