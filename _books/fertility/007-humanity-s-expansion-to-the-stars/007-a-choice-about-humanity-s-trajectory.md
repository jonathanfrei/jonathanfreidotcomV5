---
title: A choice about humanity’s trajectory
---

1. A healthy growing population is not sufficient for space expansion, but sustained demographic contraction may make it impossible.
2. The future of human life on Earth and beyond depends on whether societies can make family formation compatible with modern life.
3. The stars are not merely a destination for humanity; they are a test of whether humanity still possesses the confidence, vitality, and generational depth to build a future larger than the present.
