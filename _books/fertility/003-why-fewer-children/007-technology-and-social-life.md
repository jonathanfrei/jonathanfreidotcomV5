Technology changes several earlier thresholds: where people meet, how many alternatives remain visible, how solitary time is filled, and whether sexual desire draws someone toward another person. The measured changes, plausible mechanisms, and emerging possibilities are not equally certain.
{: .lede}

The internet arrived after fertility had already fallen substantially in many rich countries. It may intensify delay; it did not originate the broader decline.

## What is observed

Online dating has become a normal way to meet. In 2022, 30 percent of American adults reported having used a dating site or app. These tools enlarge a thin local marriage market and create relationships that would never have formed through a workplace, school, congregation, or group of friends.

Social isolation and sexual inactivity are also observed, although neither can be assigned wholly to technology. The U.S. Surgeon General's 2023 advisory assembled evidence of declining participation in several forms of in-person connection and treated loneliness and isolation as public-health concerns. Analysis of the General Social Survey found that in 2016–2018, 30.9 percent of men and 19.1 percent of women ages eighteen to twenty-four reported no sexual activity in the previous year. The increase was concentrated mainly among unmarried men and was associated with student status and, for men, weaker employment.

Japan offers a different window into the same early threshold. In the 2021 National Fertility Survey sample of never-married adults ages eighteen to thirty-four, 72.2 percent of men and 64.2 percent of women reported no current opposite-sex dating partner. The result describes that population and moment without assigning a cause or later outcome.

Many young adults are reaching family-forming ages without a partner or sexual relationship. The evidence does not assign that condition to a platform or screen.

## What is plausible

Dating platforms turn potential partners into a continuing stream of profiles. That can improve matching. It can also keep comparison active after a promising person appears. The design supplies another option more easily than it supplies trust, shared purpose, or a reason to stop searching.

The mechanism is difficult to isolate. People who find apps frustrating may already face a thin marriage market; people who marry leave the platform; different services and users behave differently. Platform design makes prolonged provisional choice plausible without establishing a national fertility effect.

Streaming, games, social media, and pornography can occupy hours that previous generations had fewer ways to spend alone. No one can infer that a person watching a film would otherwise be courting a spouse. The smaller claim is enough: solitude now carries less boredom and can be sustained with fewer recurring places, awkward encounters, or compromises with another person's preferences.

Pornography separates sexual stimulation from courtship more directly. Evidence about its effect on relationships and fertility is contested and vulnerable to selection: lonely people may use more pornography rather than pornography producing the loneliness. The inherent mechanism is narrower. It offers sexual stimulation without another person's presence, consent, demands, or possibility of conception. That can fit an already delayed life without showing how large the population effect is.

## What remains a scenario

Artificial companions move still further from measured evidence. A simulated partner can be attentive, available, and tailored without requiring mutual sacrifice. It may relieve loneliness for some people or train others toward a relationship no human being can satisfy. We do not yet know whether such systems will materially alter marriage or fertility.

For now, artificial companions are an emerging scenario rather than an established cause. They still deserve notice because they are built to replace some functions of intimate companionship.

Technology can create connection and make isolation more tolerable. It can introduce a spouse or keep the field open. Its demographic importance lies less in one device than in how it changes the early years of the life-course sequence. Another year without commitment remains comfortable and reversible—until later pages of the sequence make it neither.
